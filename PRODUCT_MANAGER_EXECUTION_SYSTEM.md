# Product Manager Execution System

> **Grand Task:** Ship 12 Major Features, Maintain 99.9% Uptime, 70+ NPS

---

## Role Overview

The Product Manager owns the product roadmap, customer research, feature prioritisation, and product metrics for Zenna. They are the voice of the customer inside the company and the voice of the product to customers.

### Core Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Roadmap Ownership | Define and maintain the 12-month product roadmap |
| Feature Prioritisation | Prioritise features by customer impact, revenue, and complexity |
| Customer Research | Conduct weekly calls with 5+ customers to understand pain points |
| Acceptance Criteria | Define clear acceptance criteria for every feature |
| Product Metrics | Own NPS, feature adoption, and churn-due-to-missing-features metrics |

---

## Daily Tasks (40 hrs/week)

### Customer Research — 5 hrs/week
- 3–4 customer calls (30–45 min each)
- Understand pain points, feature requests, workflows
- Synthesise findings into feature briefs
- Update product roadmap based on feedback

### Product Strategy — 10 hrs/week
- Design new features (user stories, wireframes, acceptance criteria)
- Prioritise backlog by revenue impact, customer request volume, and complexity
- Write feature specs with detailed requirements for engineers
- Review prototypes and mockups with customers

### Product Metrics — 5 hrs/week
- **Daily:** Feature adoption rates, NPS trending
- **Weekly:** Churn analysis (why are customers leaving?)
- **Monthly:** Feature impact (did this feature reduce churn? increase MRR?)
- **Quarterly:** Product health (are we building the right things?)

### Cross-Functional Alignment — 10 hrs/week
- Daily standup with engineering (15 min)
- Weekly review with marketing (new feature messaging)
- Weekly review with sales (customer feedback)
- Monthly review with customer success (support volume trends)

---

## Weekly Tasks

### Feature Kickoff — 2 hrs
- Review completed features with engineering
- Prioritise next sprint features
- Adjust roadmap if needed (customer feedback > original plan)

### Customer Advisory Board Call — 1 hr
- Meet with top 5–10 customers
- Show roadmap, get feedback
- Celebrate customer wins
- Prioritise next feature based on customer vote

---

## Monthly Tasks

### Product Review — 3 hrs
- NPS trend: going up or down?
- Feature adoption: are customers using new features?
- Churn rate: did product changes reduce churn?
- Support volume: are we reducing support load?

### Competitive Analysis — 2 hrs
- Check competitors for new features
- Identify gaps in Zenna roadmap
- Document benchmark features

---

## Quarterly Tasks

### Roadmap Planning — 4 hrs
- **Q1:** MVP features (call answering, booking, deposits, calendar sync)
- **Q2:** Expansion features (multi-location, team management, analytics)
- **Q3:** Integration features (API, white-label, partner ecosystem)
- **Q4:** Advanced features (AI customisation, predictive analytics, adjacent products)
