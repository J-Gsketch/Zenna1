# Expansion Playbook

> Upsell, cross-sell, and land-and-expand strategies to grow revenue from existing customers.

---

## Expansion Philosophy

**The cheapest revenue is expansion revenue.** A customer who already trusts Zenna is 5–10x more likely to buy additional products than a new prospect. Our goal: **10%+ of MRR from expansion by Month 6.**

---

## Expansion Opportunities

### 1. Tier Upgrade (Starter → Pro → Enterprise)

| Trigger | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Usage exceeds plan limits | >80% of monthly call allowance | "You're growing! Here's what Pro unlocks" |
| Feature request matches higher tier | Asks about team management | "That's available in Pro — want a demo?" |
| Business growth | Hires more staff, new location | "Congrats on the growth! Let's talk about multi-location" |
| Time-based | 3+ months on current plan | Quarterly business review with upgrade pitch |

### 2. Add-On Products

| Add-On | Target Customer | Price | Value Prop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-location | Businesses with 2+ locations | +$50/mo | Unified dashboard, per-location AI |
| Team management | Businesses with 3+ staff | +$30/mo | Per-tech calendars, role-based access |
| Analytics | Data-driven owners | +$20/mo | Revenue attribution, call analytics |
| White-label | Agencies, franchises | Custom | Rebrand Zenna as their own |
| API access | Tech-savvy businesses | +$40/mo | Custom integrations |

### 3. Seat Expansion (Land-and-Expand)

1. **Land:** Sign up the business owner (1 seat)
2. **Expand:** Add office manager (2nd seat)
3. **Expand:** Add lead technician (3rd seat)
4. **Expand:** Add all technicians (5+ seats)
5. **Expand:** Upgrade to Enterprise (unlimited seats)

## Upsell Playbooks

### Playbook 1: Usage-Based Upgrade

**Scenario:** Customer is consistently hitting their plan limits.

**Script:**
> "Hey [Name], I noticed Zenna answered [X] calls for you last month — that's fantastic! You're actually at [Y]% of your plan limit. I wanted to chat about upgrading to [Plan] so you never miss a call. You'd also get [feature 1] and [feature 2]. Want me to walk you through it?"

**Timing:** When usage hits 80% for 2 consecutive weeks.

### Playbook 2: Feature-Driven Upgrade

**Scenario:** Customer requests a feature available in a higher tier.

**Script:**
> "Great news — [feature] is available in our [Plan] plan! It's $[X]/month and also includes [bonus feature]. Given how much you're already using Zenna, I think it's a no-brainer. Want me to set up a trial of [Plan] so you can test it?"

**Timing:** Same day as feature request.

### Playbook 3: Business Growth Upgrade

**Scenario:** Customer's business is growing (hired staff, new location, more calls).

**Script:**
> "Congrats on the growth! I noticed you've [hired 2 new techs / opened a new location]. Zenna can grow with you — our [Plan] includes [relevant features]. A few of our customers in similar situations have seen [specific benefit]. Can I show you how it works?"

**Timing:** When customer shares growth news.

### Playbook 4: Quarterly Business Review Upsell

**Agenda for QBR with expansion pitch:**
1. **Celebrate wins (10 min):** Calls answered, bookings, ROI
2. **Review usage (5 min):** Current plan utilisation
3. **Identify gaps (5 min):** "What else could Zenna do for you?"
4. **Present upgrade (10 min):** Tailored pitch based on usage and goals
5. **Trial offer:** "Try [Plan] free for 14 days — no commitment"

## Land-and-Expand Strategy

### Phase 1: Land (Month 1)
- Sign up business owner on Starter plan
- Focus on core value: call answering + booking
- Goal: Prove ROI quickly

### Phase 2: Expand Usage (Month 2–3)
- Increase call volume through Zenna
- Add deposit collection
- Introduce analytics
- Goal: Become indispensable

### Phase 3: Expand Seats (Month 3–6)
- Add team members to the account
- Introduce team management features
- Goal: Multi-user dependency

### Phase 4: Expand Products (Month 6–12)
- Upgrade to Pro or Enterprise
- Add multi-location, API, white-label
- Goal: Maximum revenue per customer
