# Partnership Development Playbook

> Channel strategy, partner identification, negotiation, and ecosystem management.

---

## Partnership Vision

**Goal:** $50K+ ARR from partnerships by Month 12, with 3–5 active partners generating 30%+ of new revenue.

---

## Partnership Types

### 1. White-Label Partners
**What:** Partner rebrands Zenna as their own product and sells to their customers.

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Target | Trade software companies, marketing agencies, franchise groups |
| Revenue Model | Revenue share (70/30 partner/Zenna) or wholesale pricing |
| Support Level | Zenna provides backend, partner provides frontend support |
| Examples | Trade-specific CRM adding AI receptionist feature |

### 2. Affiliate Partners
**What:** Partner refers customers to Zenna and earns commission.

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Target | Industry influencers, trade associations, business coaches |
| Revenue Model | 20% recurring commission for 12 months |
| Support Level | Self-serve with marketing materials |
| Examples | Plumbing business coach recommending Zenna to clients |

### 3. Reseller Partners
**What:** Partner sells Zenna alongside their existing services.

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Target | IT service providers, telecom resellers, business consultants |
| Revenue Model | 30% margin on subscription |
| Support Level | Partner provides first-line support, Zenna provides escalation |
| Examples | Managed IT provider bundling Zenna with phone systems |

### 4. Integration Partners
**What:** Technical integration with complementary software.

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Target | Housecall Pro, JobTracker, MYOB, Xero, ServiceM8 |
| Revenue Model | Co-marketing, mutual referrals |
| Support Level | Technical integration, joint documentation |
| Examples | Zenna booking syncs with ServiceM8 job management |

## Partnership Pitch

### Initial Outreach

```
Subject: Partnership opportunity — AI receptionist for [Partner]'s customers

G'day [Name],

I'm [Name], founder of Zenna — an AI receptionist built specifically for 
Australian trade businesses. We help plumbers, electricians, and HVAC 
companies answer 100% of their calls and book jobs automatically.

I think there's a natural fit between [Partner] and Zenna:
- [Specific reason, e.g., "Your customers use ServiceM8 for job management 
  but still miss calls when they're on the tools"]

Would you be open to a 20-minute call to explore how we could work together?

Cheers,
[Your name]
```

### Partnership Proposal Deck (Key Slides)

1. **Zenna overview:** What we do, traction, metrics
2. **Partnership opportunity:** Why it makes sense for both companies
3. **Customer overlap:** Shared customer profile
4. **Revenue model:** How both parties make money
5. **Integration plan:** Technical approach (if applicable)
6. **Go-to-market:** Joint marketing plan
7. **Timeline:** Milestones and launch date
8. **Next steps:** What we need from each other

## Negotiation Framework

### Key Terms to Negotiate

| Term | Our Target | Fallback | Walk Away |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue share | 70/30 (us/them) | 60/40 | 50/50 |
| Exclusivity | Non-exclusive | Category-exclusive | Fully exclusive |
| Minimum commitment | $1K/mo | $500/mo | No minimum |
| Contract length | 12 months | 6 months | 3 months |
| Marketing commitment | Joint launch + quarterly | Joint launch only | None |
| Support SLA | 24 hr response | 48 hr response | Best effort |

### Deal Structure

```
Partnership Agreement: Zenna × [Partner]

Type: [White-label / Affiliate / Reseller / Integration]
Revenue Model: [X]% revenue share / [X]% commission / Wholesale pricing
Minimum Commitment: $[X]/month
Contract Term: [X] months
Exclusivity: [Non-exclusive / Category-exclusive / Exclusive]

Zenna Provides:
- AI receptionist platform and infrastructure
- Technical integration support
- Marketing materials and co-branding assets
- Partner dashboard for tracking referrals/revenue
- Dedicated partner success manager

Partner Provides:
- Access to their customer base
- [Marketing/sales/integration support]
- First-line customer support (if reseller/white-label)
- Quarterly business reviews
```

## Partner Onboarding

### Week 1: Setup
- [ ] Sign partnership agreement
- [ ] Create partner account in Zenna
- [ ] Provide demo account and API keys (if applicable)
- [ ] Share marketing materials (logos, screenshots, case studies)

### Week 2: Training
- [ ] 1-hour product training call
- [ ] 1-hour sales training call
- [ ] Provide sales playbook and objection handling guide
- [ ] Set up tracking (referral links, promo codes, API integration)

### Week 3: Launch
- [ ] Joint announcement (blog post, email, social media)
- [ ] Partner sends first promotion to their audience
- [ ] Zenna features partner on website
- [ ] Monitor first referrals/signups

### Ongoing: Support
- [ ] Monthly partner check-in call
- [ ] Quarterly business review
- [ ] Dedicated Slack channel for quick questions
- [ ] Co-marketing calendar planning
