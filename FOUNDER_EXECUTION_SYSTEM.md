# Founder/CEO Execution System

> **Grand Task:** Raise $2–5M Series A, Build 50-Person Team, Market Leader Position

---

## Role Overview

The Founder/CEO owns strategy, vision, fundraising, hiring, key relationships, and public presence. They are the chief storyteller, chief recruiter, and chief decision-maker. They focus on the 3-year vision while the team executes the daily plan.

### Core Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Product Vision | Where is Zenna going? 3-year strategic direction |
| Fundraising | Capital strategy, investor relations, pitch process |
| Hiring & Culture | Recruit top talent, build winning team, define values |
| Strategic Partnerships | Negotiate major deals, align ecosystem |
| Public Presence | Thought leadership, founder brand, press |
| M&A Exploration | Acquisition targets, investor interest, exit strategy |

---

## Daily Tasks (30–40 hrs/week)

### Strategy & Vision — 10 hrs/week
- Think about 3-year vision (where should Zenna be in 2027?)
- Quarterly planning (goals, OKRs for entire company)
- Weekly executive review (are we on track?)
- Customer research (quarterly founder sales calls, advisory board)

### Fundraising — 5 hrs/week (if actively fundraising)
- Investor meetings (pitch, answer questions, build relationships)
- Pitch deck refinement (based on investor feedback)
- Financial model updates (show path to profitability)
- Legal documents (term sheet review, advice from counsel)

### Hiring & Culture — 10 hrs/week
- Recruitment (meet candidates, make hiring decisions)
- Onboarding (first week at Zenna, set expectations)
- 1-on-1s (monthly with direct reports)
- Culture building (values, team rituals, celebration)

### Strategic Partnerships — 5 hrs/week
- Identify potential partners (CEO calls with Housecall Pro, JobTracker, MYOB)
- Negotiate deals (revenue share, white-label terms)
- Celebrate milestones with partners (joint press releases, co-marketing)

### Public Presence — 5 hrs/week
- Podcast interviews (pitch outreach, prep, record)
- Speaking gigs (trade conferences, investor events)
- LinkedIn/Twitter (share company wins, thought leadership)
- Press outreach (pitch to tech + trade publications)

---

## Weekly Tasks

### Executive Team Sync — 1.5 hrs
- All-hands check-in (marketing, engineering, product, customer success, sales, ops)
- Celebrate wins
- Align on priorities
- Resolve blockers

### Board Updates (optional, if funded)
- Weekly email to investors (progress, metrics, asks)
- Celebrate customer wins
- Flag any concerns early

### 1-on-1s with Direct Reports — 2 hrs
- 30–45 min per direct report
- Focus: what's blocking you? what do you need? how are you feeling?
- Career development discussion

---

## Monthly Tasks

### Board Reporting — 2 hrs (if funded)
- Monthly board slide deck (metrics, highlights, risks)
- Investor updates (use this to stay in front of VCs)
- Financial review (MRR, burn, runway, profitability timeline)

### Strategy Review — 2 hrs
- Are we tracking to quarterly goals?
- Any strategic pivots needed?
- Next quarter priorities?
- Culture check-in (team morale, retention risk?)

### Hiring Review — 1 hr
- Next role to hire?
- Candidate pipeline (recruiting progress)
- Compensation benchmarking (are we competitive?)

---

## Quarterly Tasks

### Strategic Planning — 6 hrs
- **Q1:** MVP proof of concept, founder-led sales, team hiring begins
- **Q2:** Product-market fit, marketing scaling, Series A prep
- **Q3:** Series A fundraising (if pursuing), team growth, expansion strategy
- **Q4:** Series A close, team building, next-year vision planning

### Fundraising (if applicable)
- Series A target: raise $2–5M
- Investor outreach: build pipeline of 20+ VCs
- Data room preparation: cap table, financial models, customer agreements
- Legal counsel: term sheet review, due diligence prep

### M&A Exploration — 1–2 hrs/quarter
- Who could acquire Zenna? (larger trade software companies)
- Who could Zenna acquire? (complementary products, teams)
- What's the strategic value?

## Founder Wellness & Sustainability

> **The #1 risk to Zenna is founder burnout.** Protect yourself.

| Practice | Frequency | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Exercise | 4x/week | Energy, clarity, stress relief |
| Sleep | 7–8 hrs/night | Decision quality, creativity |
| Meditation/journaling | Daily | Mental clarity, emotional regulation |
| Non-work hobbies | Weekly | Perspective, creativity, identity beyond Zenna |
| Peer group/mentor | Monthly | "It's lonely at the top" — have someone to talk to |
| Vacation | 1 week/quarter | Recharge, strategic thinking |
