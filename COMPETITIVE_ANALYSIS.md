## 3. Zenna's Unfair Advantages & Positioning

1. **Founder Credibility & Language:** Zenna speaks tradie language ("call-out fee", "elbows deep", "on-site", "mate"). No corporate jargon.
2. **Zero Technical Overhead:** No Zapier, no complex API webhooks, no prompt engineering required. 5-minute wizard gets tradies live.
3. **Flat-Rate Predictable Pricing:** $99/mo flat rate instead of anxiety-inducing per-minute billing.
4. **Local AU/NZ Infrastructure:** Native support for Australian (+61) and New Zealand (+64) phone numbers and regional accents.

---

## 4. Sales Battlecards

### Battlecard 1: How to Win Against Legacy Human Answering Services (Ruby / AnswerConnect)
- **Their Weakness:** Expensive ($350–$600/month), per-minute billing penalties, operators don't know trade terms, slow transfer times (45–60s).
- **Zenna Winning Pitch:** *"Why pay $400 a month for a call center operator who reads off a script and doesn't know what a call-out fee is? Zenna answers in 3 seconds for $99/month flat, quotes your exact pricing, and texts booking links directly."*

---

### Battlecard 2: How to Win Against Developer AI Platforms (Bland AI / Retell AI / Synthflow)
- **Their Weakness:** Built for software developers, requires API coding, complex webhooks, unpredictable usage billing.
- **Zenna Winning Pitch:** *"Tools like Bland or Retell are building blocks for developers. Tradies don't want to write code or configure WebRTC streams. Zenna is a turn-key solution configured for your plumbing business in 5 minutes flat."*

---

### Battlecard 3: Position Relative to Tradie CRMs (ServiceM8 / Jobber)
- **Positioning:** Zenna **complements** ServiceM8/Jobber—we don't compete with them.
- **Zenna Winning Pitch:** *"ServiceM8 manages your jobs after they are booked. Zenna sits in front of ServiceM8 to catch the initial missed call and push the booked customer directly into your schedule."*
