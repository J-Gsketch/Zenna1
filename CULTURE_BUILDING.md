# Culture Building

> Team rituals, values, communication norms, and growth practices for Zenna.

---

## Our Values

### 1. Customer Obsessed
- We build for Dave the plumber, not for VCs or tech Twitter
- Every decision starts with: "Does this help our customers?"
- We talk to customers every week, not just when something breaks

### 2. Ship Fast
- Done is better than perfect
- Launch, learn, iterate — don't polish in private
- If you're not slightly embarrassed by v1, you shipped too late

### 3. Radical Transparency
- Share metrics, decisions, and failures openly
- Everyone sees the dashboard (MRR, churn, runway)
- Post-mortems are blameless — focus on systems, not people

### 4. Owner Mindset
- Act like you own the company (because you do — equity)
- See a problem? Fix it. Don't wait for permission.
- Spend company money like it's your own

### 5. No Ego
- Best idea wins, regardless of title
- Disagree openly, commit fully once decided
- Feedback is a gift — give it directly, receive it gracefully

### 6. Sustainable Pace
- Marathon, not sprint. We build for 10 years, not 10 weeks.
- Rest is productive. Take your leave. Log off on weekends.
- Burnout helps no one — not you, not the team, not the customer

## Communication Norms

### Slack

| Channel | Purpose | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| `#general` | Company-wide announcements | 24 hrs |
| `#engineering` | Technical discussion, PRs, deploys | 4 hrs |
| `#product` | Feature discussion, customer feedback | 4 hrs |
| `#sales` | Pipeline, deals, customer interactions | 4 hrs |
| `#customer-success` | Customer issues, onboarding, churn | 2 hrs |
| `#random` | Non-work chat, memes, fun | Whenever |
| `#incidents` | Production incidents (P0/P1) | Immediate |

### Async-First Principles
1. **Write it down** — default to written communication (Slack, Notion, docs)
2. **No expectation of immediate response** — respect deep work
3. **Meetings are for discussion, not updates** — share updates async
4. **Record meetings** — for team members in different timezones
5. **Default to open** — public channels > DMs (unless personal)

### Meeting Guidelines
- Every meeting has an agenda (shared before)
- Every meeting has a decision or outcome (documented after)
- Default meeting length: 25 or 50 minutes (not 30 or 60)
- Camera optional (respect different work environments)
- Start on time, end early

## Remote Work Culture

### Principles
- **Remote-first:** Not remote-friendly. Remote-first.
- **Output > hours:** We measure results, not time online
- **Overlap hours:** 10am–2pm AEST for synchronous collaboration
- **Documentation:** If it's not written down, it doesn't exist
- **Overcommunicate:** Share context liberally, assume nothing

### Home Office Setup
- Company provides: laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset
- $50/month internet stipend
- $500 one-time home office setup budget

### Staying Connected
- **Virtual coffee:** Random 15-min pair chats (weekly, opt-in)
- **Slack social:** `#random` for non-work chat
- **Quarterly offsite:** In-person team building (all expenses paid)
- **Co-working stipend:** $200/month if you prefer a co-working space

## Growth & Development

### Learning Budget
- $1,000/year per person for courses, books, conferences
- No approval needed for <$200 purchases
- Share learnings with the team (blog post, Slack summary, lunch & learn)

- Cross-functional projects encouraged

## Diversity & Inclusion

### Commitments
- We hire the best person for the role, regardless of background
- We actively source from underrepresented groups
- We use inclusive language in all communications
- We accommodate different working styles and needs
- We have zero tolerance for discrimination or harassment

### Practices
- Inclusive job descriptions (no unnecessary requirements)
- Diverse interview panels
- Structured interviews (same questions for all candidates)
- Regular D&I training
- Safe reporting channel for concerns
