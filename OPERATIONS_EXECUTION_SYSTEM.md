# Operations Execution System

> **Grand Task:** Profitable Unit Economics, Healthy Runway, Compliant Infrastructure

---

## Role Overview

The Operations Manager/CFO owns financial tracking, accounting, compliance, hiring, and business infrastructure for Zenna. They ensure the business is financially healthy, legally compliant, and operationally efficient.

### Core Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Financial Tracking | Burn rate, runway, profitability timeline |
| Accounting & Bookkeeping | Invoicing, vendor payments, bank reconciliation |
| Compliance | ABN, tax (GST, income tax, superannuation), employment law |
| Hiring & HR | Recruitment, onboarding, payroll, performance management |
| Business Infrastructure | Contracts, insurance, legal, vendor management |

---

## Daily Tasks (30 hrs/week)

### Financial Tracking — 8 hrs/week
- **Daily:** Track cash flow (money in, money out)
- **Weekly:** MRR calculation (recurring revenue from Stripe)
- **Weekly:** Expense review (is spending aligned with budget?)
- **Weekly:** Burn rate monitoring (how much runway do we have?)

### Accounting — 8 hrs/week
- Invoice customers (manage Stripe subscriptions, handle billing exceptions)
- Pay vendors (Twilio, SendGrid, Firebase, salaries)
- Reconcile bank account (ensure nothing fraudulent)
- Tax preparation (calculate quarterly tax, prepare for accountant)

### Compliance — 6 hrs/week
- ABN registry updates (maintain company registration)
- Tax compliance (GST, income tax, superannuation)
- Employment law (payroll, leave, contractor agreements)
- Insurance (public liability, cyber, professional indemnity)

### HR & Hiring — 8 hrs/week
- Recruitment (job postings, candidate screening, interviews)
- Onboarding (paperwork, systems access, training)
- Payroll (calculate salary, benefits, tax withholding)
- Performance management (quarterly reviews, feedback)

---

## Weekly Tasks

### Financial Review — 2 hrs
- MRR trend (is it growing?)
- Burn rate (can we sustain for another quarter?)
- Runway (when do we run out of money?)
- Profitability timeline (when do we break even?)

### Cash Management — 1 hr
- Review outstanding invoices (any customers not paying?)
- Pay bills (on time, to maintain vendor relationships)
- Track payroll (ensure accurate, on-time salary payments)

---

## Monthly Tasks

### Financial Statement — 2 hrs
- P&L (revenue, expenses, profit/loss)
- Balance sheet (assets, liabilities, equity)
- Cash flow (money in/out, runway projection)
- Variance analysis (budget vs. actual, explain differences)

### Tax & Compliance — 1 hr
- Quarterly tax calculation (ensure we're tracking tax liability)
- Superannuation contributions (for employees)
- Payroll reconciliation (salaries, tax withheld)

### Vendor Management — 1 hr
- Review vendor contracts (Stripe rates, Firebase pricing)
- Negotiate better terms (bulk discount? longer commitment?)
- Audit spending (are we using all these tools?)

---

## Quarterly Tasks

### Business Planning — 4 hrs
- Budget for next quarter (expected revenue, planned expenses)
- Hiring plan (when to hire next team member?)
- Financial forecast (runway extended? profitability timeline?)
- Strategic review (are we on track for yearly goals?)

### Compliance Audit — 2 hrs
- ABN & tax registration up to date?
- Insurance adequate for business size?
- Employment contracts compliant?
- Data protection & GDPR compliant? (if serving EU customers)

## Key Financial Ratios

### Unit Economics

```
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) = Total Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers
LTV (Lifetime Value) = ARPU × Gross Margin × Average Customer Lifespan
LTV:CAC Ratio = LTV / CAC (target: 3:1 or higher)
CAC Payback = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin) (target: <6 months)
```

### Burn Rate & Runway

```
Monthly Burn Rate = Total Monthly Expenses − Total Monthly Revenue
Runway (months) = Cash On Hand / Monthly Burn Rate
```

### Profitability Timeline

```
Break-Even Month = Month when Monthly Revenue ≥ Monthly Expenses
Track monthly: Revenue vs. Expenses chart
```
