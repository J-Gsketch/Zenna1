# Zenna Go-To-Market (GTM) Playbook: 0 → 200+ Paying Tradie Customers

> **Strategy Focus:** Founder-led community growth, word-of-mouth referral loops, and direct operator credibility across Australia and New Zealand trade markets.

---

## 1. GTM Strategy Overview

Early SaaS category leaders like Stripe (developer communities), Calendly (viral invite loops), and Zapier (integration ecosystem) scaled rapidly by embedding themselves into their users' existing daily workflows.

Zenna's GTM playbook adapts these proven mechanics for the **trade services market**:
1. **Community-First:** Build trust in trade forums (Facebook Groups, Reddit) without aggressive sales pitches.
2. **Double-Sided Viral Loop:** Tradie-to-tradie referral engine offering $50 cash credit for both referee and referrer.
3. **Operator PR:** Founder credibility as an insider who understands job-site friction, not a distant tech company.

---

## 2. Phase 1A: Founder-Led Community Engagement (Weeks 1-2)

### Target Community Matrix
- **Facebook Groups:** *Plumbers Australia* (18k members), *Electricians Australia* (24k members), *Small Business Tradies AU/NZ* (35k members).
- **Reddit:** `r/Plumbing`, `r/Electricians`, `r/Trades`, `r/Entrepreneur`.
- **LinkedIn:** Trade Business Owners Australia, Plumbing Industry Network.
- **YouTube:** Plumbing/Electrical trade tips channels (*The Site Shed*, *Tradie Magazine*).

### The "Listen & Help First" Protocol
1. **Week 1 (Zero Self-Promotion):**
   - Comment on 5-10 posts daily answering business/call management queries.
   - Provide real advice: "Here's how I set up call forwarding when I'm on a big pour."
2. **Week 2 (Contextual Solution Sharing):**
   - Share free resources (e.g. "Free Call-Out Fee Calculator spreadsheet").
   - Mention Zenna only when directly asked: *"Disclosure: I built Zenna to fix this exact problem because I was losing $3k/wk on-site."*

## 4. Phase 1C: Social Proof Flywheel (Weeks 3-4)

### 1. iPhone Video Testimonials (First 5 Customers)
- **Guidelines:** 30–60 second raw video shot on iPhone (no glossy agency production—tradies trust raw authenticity).
- **Core Script:**
  1. *"What trade are you in and where are you based?"*
  2. *"What was your biggest headache before Zenna?"*
  3. *"How many calls or jobs has Zenna saved you this month?"*
- **Placement:** Front-and-center on landing page hero section and Facebook Ad rotation.

### 2. Live Social Proof Badges
- Dynamic customer counter widget embedded on marketing site:
  `"🔥 48 Plumbers & Electricians in AU/NZ currently using Zenna"`
- Verified reviews badge: `4.9 / 5 Stars based on 34 Tradie Reviews`.
