# Customer Success Execution System

> **Grand Task:** <2% Churn, 70+ NPS, 90%+ Onboarding Success Rate

---

## Role Overview

The Customer Success Manager (CSM) owns the entire post-sale customer journey — from onboarding through retention, expansion, and advocacy. They are the customer's champion inside Zenna.

### Core Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | Guide new customers to first value within 5 minutes, full setup within 30 days |
| Proactive Outreach | Weekly check-ins, feature education, usage tips |
| Churn Prevention | Identify at-risk customers early, intervene with targeted support |
| Expansion Sales | Upsell higher tiers, cross-sell add-on products |
| Health Scoring | Predictive churn alerts based on usage patterns |

---

## Daily Tasks (40 hrs/week)

### Onboarding New Customers — 10 hrs/week
- **Day 1:** Welcome email, send onboarding materials
- **Day 3:** Check-in call (how's setup going? any blockers?)
- **Day 7:** First test call together, celebrate the win
- **Day 14:** Business review (how many calls? leads booked? ROI?)
- **Day 30:** Win-back review (upgrade to paid or churn risk?)

### Proactive Outreach — 10 hrs/week
- **Monday:** Send weekly "Zenna wins" email (customer stories, product tips)
- **Wednesday:** One-on-one calls with top 5 customers (what's working?)
- **Friday:** Send week-ahead tips (feature deep-dive, use case ideas)

### Churn Prevention — 10 hrs/week
- **Daily:** Identify at-risk customers (low usage, no logins in 5 days)
- Send "We Miss You" email (offer help, show new features)
- If high-value customer: schedule call to understand why
- Option: offer discount (20% off, 2 weeks free) to retain

### Health Monitoring — 10 hrs/week
- **Daily:** Review usage metrics (active customers, calls per customer)
- **Weekly:** Identify expansion opportunities (customer on Starter, but usage suggests Pro)
- **Weekly:** Churn risk dashboard (red flags: low usage, support complaints, feature requests unfulfilled)

---

## Weekly Tasks

### NPS Survey Campaign — 1 hr
- Send NPS survey to 20–30 customers
- Collect and categorise feedback
- Follow up with detractors (score 0–6): "What would make you a 10?"
- Celebrate promoters (score 9–10): "Would you be willing to do a testimonial?"

### Team Sync — 1 hr
- Review customer feedback with product team
- Share churn reasons (features? price? support?)
- Celebrate customer wins in company standup

---

## Monthly Tasks

### Health Review — 2 hrs
- Analyse churn: did we lose customers? why?
- Analyse retention: what's the cohort retention curve?
- Expansion revenue: how much revenue from upsells?
- NPS trend: improving or declining?

### Customer Advocacy — 1 hr
- Select 2–3 customers for case studies
- Request testimonial videos
- Feature customer story on social media
- Invite customer to speak at virtual event

---

## Quarterly Tasks

### Retention Strategies — 3 hrs
- **Q1:** Nail onboarding (get customers to "aha moment" fast)
- **Q2:** Expand to power users (identify most successful, help others replicate)
- **Q3:** Community building (customer Facebook group, user webinars)
- **Q4:** Loyalty program (reward long-term customers, referral incentives)

## Customer Health Score

### Scoring Model

| Signal | Weight | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Login frequency | 25% | Daily | Weekly | 5+ days |
| Call volume | 25% | 10+/week | 5–9/week | <5/week |
| Feature usage | 20% | 3+ features | 2 features | 1 feature |
| Support tickets | 15% | 0–1/month | 2–3/month | 4+/month |
| NPS score | 15% | 9–10 | 7–8 | 0–6 |

### Health Score Actions

| Score | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 80–100 | Healthy | Explore expansion, request referral |
| 60–79 | Monitor | Proactive check-in call |
| 40–59 | At Risk | Schedule intervention, offer help |
| 0–39 | Critical | Founder call, retention offer, save plan |
