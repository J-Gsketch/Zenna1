# Sales Execution System

> **Grand Task:** $100K+ ARR from Enterprise + $50K ARR from Partnerships

---

## Role Overview

The Sales Lead owns enterprise deal closing, channel partnership development, pipeline management, and sales metrics for Zenna. They are responsible for growing revenue through direct sales and strategic partnerships.

### Core Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Sales | Close deals with $5K–20K ACV (multi-location trade businesses) |
| Channel Development | Build partnerships (white-label, affiliates, resellers) |
| Pipeline Management | Maintain 3x pipeline coverage, accurate forecasting |
| Contract Negotiation | Customise pricing, terms, and contracts for enterprise deals |
| Sales Metrics | Own pipeline value, close rate, ACV, and revenue targets |

---

## Daily Tasks (40 hrs/week)

### Enterprise Sales — 20 hrs/week
- **Prospecting:** Identify 10–20 high-value targets (multi-location plumbing companies, large electrical contractors)
- **Outreach:** Personalised emails, LinkedIn messages (5–10 touches/week)
- **Demo calls:** Walk through Zenna with qualified leads
- **Negotiation:** Customise pricing and contract terms for enterprise deals
- **Close:** Manage deal from proposal to signed contract

### Pipeline Management — 10 hrs/week
- Update CRM daily (Airtable, HubSpot, or Salesforce)
- Track deal stage: Prospecting → Demo → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed
- Forecast next month's closings (pipeline value × close rate)
- Identify at-risk deals (no movement in 2 weeks = follow up)

### Channel Development — 10 hrs/week
- Research potential partners (resellers, agencies, software integrators)
- Pitch partnership terms (white-label, affiliate commissions, channel support)
- Onboard partners (provide demo accounts, marketing materials)
- Support partners (help them close deals, track their success)

---

## Weekly Tasks

### Sales Standup — 1 hr
- Pipeline review: total opportunity value, close rate
- Celebrate wins: customer closed, partnership signed
- Address blockers: deal stuck? need founder help?

### Territory Planning — 1 hr
- Identify next 20 targets (focus on 2–3 geographic regions or customer segments)
- Research decision makers (LinkedIn, company websites)
- Queue outreach for coming week

---

## Monthly Tasks

### Sales Review — 2 hrs
- Actual vs. forecast (did we hit the pipeline target?)
- Close rate analysis (what % of demos convert?)
- ACV trend (are we selling bigger or smaller deals?)
- Churn analysis (do enterprise customers stay? why do they leave?)

### Contract & Legal — 1 hr
- Ensure all closed deals are properly documented
- Send contracts to accounting for invoicing
- Update CRM (close date, contract value, renewal date)

---

## Quarterly Tasks

### Sales Strategy — 3 hrs
- **Q1:** Focus on founder-led sales (close first 10 deals)
- **Q2:** Hire sales rep, ramping on product and process
- **Q3:** Sales rep closing half of deals, adding channel partners
- **Q4:** Sales rep closing majority, 3–5 partners generating 30%+ of revenue

## Sales Process

### Stage 1: Prospecting
- Identify target companies (multi-location trade businesses, 5+ employees)
- Find decision maker on LinkedIn
- Research their current call handling process
- **Exit criteria:** Qualified lead agrees to a demo

### Stage 2: Demo
- 30-minute video call walking through Zenna
- Focus on their pain points (missed calls, after-hours leads)
- Show ROI calculator: "You're losing $X/month in missed calls"
- **Exit criteria:** Prospect requests a proposal

### Stage 3: Proposal
- Customised proposal with pricing and terms
- Include case study from similar business
- Offer pilot program (30-day trial at reduced rate)
- **Exit criteria:** Proposal accepted, move to negotiation

### Stage 4: Negotiation
- Address pricing objections
- Customise contract terms (payment schedule, termination clause)
- Involve founder for deals >$10K ACV
- **Exit criteria:** Contract signed

### Stage 5: Closed-Won
- Hand off to Customer Success for onboarding
- Log deal in CRM with all details
- Celebrate in team standup
- Request referral or testimonial
