# Customer Onboarding Playbook

> Get every new customer to their "aha moment" in under 5 minutes and fully onboarded within 30 days.

---

## Onboarding Philosophy

The first 30 days determine whether a customer stays for years or churns in month 2. Our goal: **every customer experiences Zenna answering a real call and booking a real job within their first week.**

### Success Definition
A customer is "onboarded" when they have:
1. Connected their phone number (Twilio)
2. Configured their AI receptionist personality
3. Completed their first live call answered by Zenna
4. Received their first booking with deposit collected
5. Logged in at least 3 times in the first week

---

## Day 1: Welcome & Quick Start

### Automated (Immediately After Signup)
- [ ] Welcome email sent with login credentials
- [ ] Onboarding checklist displayed in dashboard
- [ ] Quick-start video (3 min) embedded in welcome modal
- [ ] AI receptionist pre-configured with industry defaults (plumbing, electrical, etc.)

### CSM Actions
- [ ] Send personalised welcome message (SMS or email)
- [ ] Review customer's business profile
- [ ] Prepare customised onboarding plan

### Customer Actions
- [ ] Log in to dashboard
- [ ] Complete business profile (name, services, service area)
- [ ] Connect Google Calendar

**Target:** Customer completes profile and connects calendar within 24 hours.

---

## Day 3: Check-In Call

### CSM Call Script (15 min)

1. **Rapport (2 min):** "G'day! How's the setup going? Any questions so far?"
2. **Progress check (5 min):** Review onboarding checklist together
3. **Blocker removal (5 min):** Help with any stuck items
4. **Next steps (3 min):** "By Day 7, we'll have Zenna answering your first call. Here's what we need..."

### Common Day 3 Blockers

| Blocker | Solution |
|---|---|
| "I don't know how to connect my phone" | Screen-share walkthrough of Twilio setup |
| "What should the AI say?" | Show industry-specific templates, customise together |
| "I'm too busy to set this up" | Offer concierge setup (we do it for them) |
| "How does the deposit work?" | Explain Stripe integration, show example payment link |

---

## Day 7: First Test Call

### Milestone: Zenna Answers a Real Call

1. **Pre-call prep (CSM):**
   - Verify Twilio number is active
   - Verify AI personality is configured
   - Verify calendar is synced
   - Verify Stripe is connected

2. **Test call (with CSM on line):**
   - CSM or customer calls the Twilio number
   - Zenna answers, qualifies the caller
   - Review the call recording together
   - Adjust AI responses if needed

3. **Celebrate:**
   - "Zenna just answered her first call for [Business Name]! 🎉"
   - Post in customer Slack/Facebook group
   - Send congratulatory email

**Target:** 90%+ of customers complete first test call by Day 7.

---

## Day 14: Business Review

### Review Call (30 min)

| Topic | Questions |
|---|---|
| Call volume | "How many calls has Zenna answered this week?" |
| Lead quality | "Are the leads qualified? Any tyre-kickers getting through?" |
| Bookings | "How many bookings? Any deposits collected?" |
| ROI | "How much time has Zenna saved you this week?" |
| Feedback | "What's working? What's frustrating?" |

### Success Criteria at Day 14
- [ ] 5+ calls answered by Zenna
- [ ] 2+ leads qualified
- [ ] 1+ booking made
- [ ] Customer expresses satisfaction (verbal or NPS)

### If Behind on Milestones
- Identify the blocker (technical? process? adoption?)
- Create a catch-up plan with specific actions
- Schedule follow-up call in 3 days
- Consider extending trial if needed

---

## Day 30: Win-Back Review

### Decision Point: Convert to Paid or Address Churn Risk

#### If Customer Is Happy (Green Path)
- [ ] Review usage stats and ROI
- [ ] Recommend appropriate plan (Starter → Pro if usage justifies)
- [ ] Process upgrade/continuation
- [ ] Request testimonial or referral
- [ ] Set 90-day success plan

#### If Customer Is Hesitant (Yellow Path)
- [ ] Understand the hesitation (price? features? not enough value?)
- [ ] Address specific concerns
- [ ] Offer extended trial (1 week) or discount (20% first 3 months)
- [ ] Schedule follow-up in 5 days

#### If Customer Wants to Cancel (Red Path)
- [ ] Exit interview: "What's the main reason you're leaving?"
- [ ] Offer save plan (discount, different plan, pause subscription)
- [ ] If still cancelling: process gracefully, leave door open
- [ ] Log churn reason for product team review
