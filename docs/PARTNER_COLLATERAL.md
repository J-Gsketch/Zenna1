# Partner Onboarding Collateral — Zenna by Hammer & Code

Co-marketing, reseller, and trade group partnership guidelines for **Zenna by Hammer & Code**.

---

## 1. Trade Group Partnership Model
Hammer & Code partners with trade associations (Plumbers Association, Master Electricians, Master Builders) to provide Zenna AI receptionists to member fleets.

- **Member Discount**: 15% recurring discount on Pro & Enterprise plans.
- **Dedicated Provisioning**: Bulk Twilio phone line allocation.
- **Co-Branded Portal**: Custom landing page and co-branded dashboard logo.

## 2. Recommended Pitch Script for Partners
> *"Tired of missing $3k+ jobs while on-site or under a house? Zenna by Hammer & Code is your AI receptionist. She catches your calls, qualifies your customers, charges upfront diagnostic fees, and syncs your calendar so you can focus on building."*
