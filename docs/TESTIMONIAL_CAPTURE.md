# Zenna Testimonial Capture Guide

How to get a first video testimonial from a trial or paying customer.

---

## Step 1: Identify the Right Customer

Pick a customer who has:
- Engaged actively with Zenna (booked jobs, replied to your check-ins)
- Expressed positive feedback verbally or by email
- A friendly, approachable communication style

## Step 2: Ask

Call or message them directly:

```
Hi [Name], really glad Zenna's been working well for [Business Name]!
Would you be up for a quick 1-minute video testimonial? Just you talking
about the problem it solved and the results — takes 2 minutes to film on
your phone. Happy to send you a few simple prompts to answer.
```

## Step 3: Script Prompts

Send these prompts so they know what to say (they should answer in their
own words, not read a script verbatim):

1. "What problem were you having before Zenna?"
2. "How has Zenna helped your business?" (jobs booked, time saved, calls
   captured)
3. "Would you recommend it to another tradie? Why?"

## Step 4: Recording Guidelines

- Use your phone, horizontal (landscape) video
- 60–90 seconds max
- Good lighting (natural daylight works well)
- Quiet background (avoid job sites with heavy machinery noise)
- Ask them to say their name, business name, and trade at the start

## Step 5: Usage

- Landing page (hero section or dedicated testimonials section)
- Facebook/Google ad creative
- Email campaigns (link or embedded clip)
- Social media (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels)
