# Partnership Targets

Twilio didn't build phone systems — it partnered with them. Stripe didn't build e-commerce — it powered it. Zenna should integrate and co-market, not compete.

---

|--------|-----|-------|-------|
| **JobTracker** | Their customers = Zenna's ICP | "We integrate with your platform and send you customers" | 10–20% rev share or white-label (see below) |

## White-Label Structure (Tier 1 endgame)

- License Zenna to the partner (e.g., resold as "JobTracker Receptionist").
- **Revenue split:** Zenna keeps 30% of the subscription; partner keeps 70% (they handle sales/support).
- Partner branding, Zenna backend.
- Goal: partner customers auto-upgrade to the bundle.

**Why partners say yes:** higher perceived value of their platform, new revenue line, zero R&D cost.

**Why Zenna wins:** passive distribution into thousands of existing trade businesses.
