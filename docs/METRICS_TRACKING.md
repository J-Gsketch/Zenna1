# Zenna Metrics Tracking

Daily/weekly KPI sheet for the customer acquisition campaign. Copy this
structure into a Google Sheet or spreadsheet of your choice.

## Weekly Summary Targets

| Metric | Target (Week 1) |
|--------|------------------|
| Outreach sent | 50+ |
| Responses received | 20–30 |
| Trial signups | 5–10 |
| Conversions to paid | 1–3 |
| Testimonial videos recorded | 1 |
| Revenue | $100–500 |
