# Zenna Case Study Template

Use this process to capture and publish your first customer wins.

---

## Step 1: Identify the Right Customer

Look for a customer who:
- Has been using Zenna for at least 1–2 weeks
- Has booked at least 2–3 jobs through Zenna
- Responds positively to check-in emails/calls

## Step 2: Interview Questions

Ask these on a 10–15 minute call (record with permission):

1. What problem were you having before Zenna? (missed calls, admin
   overload, after-hours coverage, etc.)
2. What made you decide to try it?
3. What was setup like?
4. How many jobs/calls has Zenna helped you capture since you started?
5. What's the estimated dollar value of those jobs?
6. How much time has it saved you per week?
7. Would you recommend Zenna to another tradie? Why?
8. Any specific moment/story where Zenna caught a job you would've missed?

## Step 3: Format as a 1-Page Case Study (PDF)

```
[Business Name] — [Trade Type], [Suburb/City]

Headline: "[Result-oriented headline, e.g. 'Plumber Books 3 More Jobs
Per Week With Zenna — $15K+ Annual Gain']"

The Challenge:
[2–3 sentences describing the problem before Zenna]

The Solution:
[2–3 sentences on how they use Zenna day to day]

The Results:
- [Metric 1, e.g. "3 additional jobs booked per week"]
- [Metric 2, e.g. "$15,000+ estimated annual revenue gain"]
- [Metric 3, e.g. "5 hours/week saved on admin"]

Quote:
"[Direct customer quote, 1–2 sentences]"
— [Name], Owner, [Business Name]
```

## Step 4: Distribute

- Add to the landing page "Customer Results" section
- Use in Facebook/Google ad creative
- Include in cold outreach and email sequences as social proof
- Share in relevant Facebook groups/Reddit (with customer's permission)
