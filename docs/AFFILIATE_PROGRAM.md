# Affiliate Program

Zapier and Calendly grew through creators and affiliates recommending them. Zenna can do the same in the tradie niche.

---

## Commission Structure

| Component | Terms |
|-----------|-------|
| **Commission** | 25–30% of the customer's first-year subscription |
| **Payout** | Monthly, via Stripe |
| **Cookie window** | 60 days |
| **Qualification** | Paid only when a referred trial converts to a paid plan (post-trial) |
| **Self-referral** | Not permitted |

**Example:** An affiliate refers a plumber who converts to Starter ($99/mo) → affiliate earns ~$300–360 over year one. A Pro ($249/mo) referral earns ~$750–900.

## Who to Recruit

| Segment | Why | Where to find them |
|---------|-----|--------------------|
| YouTube creators (trade/business niche) | Built-in tradie audience, high trust | Search "plumbing business", "tradie business tips" |
| Business coaches for tradies | Direct advisory relationship with ICP | LinkedIn, trade events |
| Accountants/bookkeepers serving trades | Trusted advisor status | Xero/MYOB advisor directories |
| Existing customers | Best advocates, authentic | In-product referral prompt + personal ask |
| Trade podcasters | Audience match | Podcast directories, guest swaps |

## Recruitment Strategy

1. **Month 2:** Set up tracking (Stripe-based referral links or a tool like Rewardful/Tolt).
2. **First 10 affiliates:** personal outreach from the founder — DM/email with a demo account.
3. **Provide a kit:** demo videos, ad copy, email swipes, logos, talking points, and a live demo login.
4. **Leaderboard:** monthly public leaderboard + bonus for top affiliate (e.g., $500 bonus at 10 conversions).
5. **Viral loop:** affiliates promoting Zenna attract other affiliates (creator audiences overlap).

## Affiliate Kit Contents

- 3 pre-written social posts (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
- 2 email swipe copies
- 1 demo video (90s) + 1 case-study video
- Brand assets (logo, screenshots, banner)
- FAQ one-pager with pricing and ROI math

## Customer Referral Program (separate, in-product)

Distinct from affiliates — simpler and instant:
- **Give $50, get $50:** referrer gets a $50 account credit; the new customer gets $50 off their first month.
- Trigger prompt after the customer's first booked job (peak delight moment).

## Tracking & Payouts

- Referral links via Stripe (or affiliate platform).
- Monthly payout run on the 1st for the prior month's conversions.
- Fraud check: payouts only after the referred customer completes their first paid month.
