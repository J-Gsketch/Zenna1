# Pricing Strategy

## Recommended Model

| Tier | Price | Calls/Month | Target Customer |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Starter** | $99/mo | 100 | Solo plumbers, electricians |
| **Pro** | $249/mo | 500 | Small 2–3 person teams |
| **Enterprise** | $499/mo | Unlimited | Larger businesses, white-label |

## Why This Pricing Wins

- **HubSpot's $50 tier** proved small businesses pay for clearly-scoped entry tiers.
- **Calendly's $10–15/mo** proved software feels cheap when ROI is obvious.
- **ServiceTitan's $199+** set the expectation that trade software costs real money.
- **Zenna's $99–499/mo** seats between affordable and premium.

**The value math to repeat everywhere:** one extra booked job (avg call-out $150+) pays for Zenna several times over. At $99/mo, a tradie needs roughly one recovered job every two months to break even — Zenna should recover several per week.

## ROI Framing (use on landing page, demo calls, ads)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Missed calls per week (typical solo tradie) | 5–10 |
| Jobs lost per month (conservative) | 4–8 |
| Avg job value | $300–$800 |
| Revenue recovered per month | $1,200–$6,400 |
| Zenna Starter cost | $99 |
| **ROI multiple** | **12–60x** |

## Alternative: Add Freemium

| Tier | Price | Calls/Month |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Free** | $0 | 10 |
| **Starter** | $99/mo | 100 |
| **Pro** | $249/mo | 500 |
| **Enterprise** | $499/mo | Unlimited |

**Pros of freemium:**
- Lower friction entry (no trial expiration anxiety).
- Longer sales cycle (months to upgrade vs days).
- Network effects (free users invite other tradies).

**Cons of freemium:**
- Attracts tire-kickers (most free users never convert).
- Support cost (free users generate support load).

## Recommendation

**Start with a 7-day free trial, no credit card required.** Add the freemium tier at Month 4 *only if* churn is high or trial-to-paid conversion is weak. This matches ServiceTitan (trial-led) while keeping HubSpot's freemium play in reserve.

## Pricing Psychology Notes

- Anchor with Enterprise ($499) so Starter ($99) feels cheap.
- Never discount below $99 — instead, add onboarding value (concierge setup, free month 1 with annual plan).
- Annual billing: offer 2 months free ($990/yr Starter) to reduce churn and improve cash flow.
- Keep pricing public and transparent — Twilio/Stripe-style "no hidden fees" is a trust signal in this market.
