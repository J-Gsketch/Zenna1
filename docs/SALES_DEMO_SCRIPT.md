# Zenna Sales & Demo Script

A structured walkthrough for demoing Zenna to a prospect, plus common
objection handling.

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Confirm prospect's business name, trade type, and suburb
- [ ] Have a test call ready to trigger live during the demo
- [ ] Know their current pain point (missed calls, no after-hours coverage,
      admin overload)
- [ ] Have the pricing tiers and Stripe checkout link ready to share

---

## Demo Script

**1. Open with the problem (1 min)**
> "Thanks for jumping on, [Name]. Before I show you Zenna, can I ask — how
> many calls do you reckon you miss in a typical week while you're on a
> job?"

Let them answer. Reinforce: "Most tradies tell me it's 1–2 jobs a week lost
just from missed calls — that's real money left on the table."

**2. Introduce Zenna (1 min)**
> "Zenna is an AI receptionist built specifically for trades. It answers
> every call, qualifies the lead (suburb, job type, urgency), quotes the
> call-out fee, and books the job straight into your calendar — all while
> you're on the tools."

**3. Live demo (5 min)**
- Place a live test call to the prospect's Zenna number.
- Walk through: greeting → lead qualification → call-out fee → booking
  confirmation → deposit invoice sent.
- Show the customer dashboard: leads captured, calls logged, bookings.

**4. Show the numbers (2 min)**
> "If Zenna saves you even 1 job a week, at an average job value of
> $[X], that's $[X * 4] a month — the Starter plan is $99/mo."

**5. Ask for the trial (1 min)**
> "Want to try it free for 7 days? No card required. I can have you set up
> in 30 minutes."

---

## Objection Handling

**"I already have a receptionist/answering service."**
> "Totally get it — Zenna isn't meant to replace a great receptionist, it's
> for the calls that come in when no one's available: nights, weekends, or
> when you're mid-job. It works alongside what you've already got."

**"Sounds expensive."**
> "Fair — let's compare to the cost of one missed job. Starter is $99/mo,
> and most tradies recover that in their first booked job. Try it free for
> 7 days and see the numbers for yourself."

**"I don't trust AI to talk to my customers."**
> "Totally understand — that's exactly why the free trial includes a live
> test call, so you can hear exactly how it sounds before a single real
> customer talks to it. You can also review every call transcript."

**"I need to think about it."**
> "No worries at all — I'll send the free trial link so it's there when
> you're ready. Mind if I check back in a few days?"
