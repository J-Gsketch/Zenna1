# Competitive Analysis: What Worked for Others

Six SaaS companies that scaled from $0 to billions — and the exact plays Zenna can adapt for the Australian/NZ trade market.

---

## 1. Calendly (Scheduling Automation)

**What they did right:**
- **Simple core problem:** "Scheduling calls is painful" → solved with 1 product.
- **Free-plan trap:** Free tier got millions of users; converted 3–5% to paid.
- **Founder narrative:** Organic credibility (celebrity users recommending it publicly).
- **Integration-first:** Worked with Gmail, Slack, Outlook, Zapier from day 1.
- **Pricing:** $10–25/mo felt cheap because the ROI was obvious (save 5 hrs/week).
- **Result:** ~$3B valuation, 10M+ users, 50%+ of enterprises use it.

**Applicable to Zenna:**
- Make "automated lead booking" so obvious the free trial sells itself.
- Integrate early with tools tradies already use (Google Calendar, Stripe).
- Get influencers (well-known tradies, business coaches) talking about Zenna.
- Price aggressively low ($99/mo) so ROI is immediate.

---

## 2. Twilio (Voice/SMS Infrastructure)

**What they did right:**
- **Developer-first:** APIs so good developers *wanted* to build on them.
- **Transparent pricing:** Pay-as-you-go ($0.0075/SMS) meant no surprises.
- **Land & expand:** Started with SMS → added voice, video, chat.
- **Partner ecosystem:** Segment, SendGrid and others built on top of Twilio.
- **Enterprise trust:** Compliance, uptime SLAs, documentation.
- **Result:** ~$32B market cap, 100K+ customers.

**Applicable to Zenna:**
- Make Zenna extensible via APIs (future partners build on top).
- Transparent pricing: $99/mo means X calls/month, no hidden fees.
- Land with lead capture → expand to job management → accounting.
- Build compliance/security from day 1, not as an afterthought.
- Target integrations with incumbents (JobTracker, MYOB, Xero).

---

## 3. Stripe (Payment Processing)

**What they did right:**
- **Developer experience:** So good developers chose Stripe over free alternatives.
- **Transparent, low pricing:** 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction — competitive and clear.
- **Global expansion:** US → EU → Asia, step by step.
- **Partner API:** Shopify, Figma, Substack built on Stripe.
- **Founder obsession:** Patrick & John lived with customers and shipped fast.
- **Result:** ~$95B valuation, powers 80%+ of online payments.

**Applicable to Zenna:**
- Build a product so good competitors consider integrating with you.
- Global roadmap: Australia first (proof) → NZ → UK → US.
- Obsess over tradie feedback (monthly calls with top 10 customers).
- Make Zenna embeddable in other platforms (white-label).
- Founder should be visible (posts, podcasts, trade events).

---

## 4. ServiceTitan (Trade Management Software)

**What they did right:**
- **Vertical-specific:** Built ONLY for plumbers/HVAC, not generic software.
- **Founder story:** Founded by people from a trades family who understood the pain.
- **Workflow automation:** Replaced paper forms and manual scheduling with one tool.
- **Ecosystem:** Integrates with QuickBooks, Google, Stripe.
- **Free trial:** 14-day free, no credit card (low friction).
- **Result:** ~$17B valuation, 300K+ users, dominant in trade software.

**Applicable to Zenna:**
- Double down on "tradie-specific" language, workflows and pain points.
- Founder credibility: building for tradies like Dave, not corporates.
- Focus on "job paperwork elimination" (details auto-saved, no manual entry).
- Build for plumbers first, then electricians — not all trades at once.
- 7–14-day free trial, no credit card required.

## 5. HubSpot (CRM + Marketing Automation)

**What they did right:**
- **Freemium model:** Free CRM attracted millions; converted to paid for advanced features.
- **Content marketing:** Blog with 500+ articles ranking #1 for "how to X" queries.
- **Community:** HubSpot Academy (free certifications) built a loyal user base.
- **Pricing ladder:** Free → $50 → $500 → $3000+ (room to grow with the customer).
- **Integration hub:** 1000+ integrations (became a platform).
- **Result:** ~$20B valuation, 180K+ paying customers.

**Applicable to Zenna:**
- Consider freemium (free: 10 calls/mo; paid: 100–500+).
- Content strategy: rank for "how plumbers get more leads".
- Education: free webinars for tradies on business growth.
- Pricing ladder: $99 (Starter) → $249 (Pro) → $499 (Enterprise).
- API marketplace (future): other software plugs into Zenna.

---

## 6. Zapier (Integration Platform)

**What they did right:**
- **API-first:** Made integrations so easy any app works with any app.
- **Founder narrative:** Started as a side project, grew to ~$5B valuation.
- **Partner advocacy:** Partners promote Zapier (mutual benefit).
- **Freemium:** Free tier got millions; upsell premium users.
- **Community:** Users create workflows, share them, become advocates.
- **Result:** ~$5B valuation, 15M+ users, 6000+ app integrations.

**Applicable to Zenna:**
- Build APIs early so other tools integrate with Zenna.
- Partner program: JobTracker promotes Zenna, and vice versa.
- User-generated content: tradies share "how I use Zenna" workflows.
- Freemium model: free for small businesses, paid for scale.
