# Zenna Customer Acquisition Playbook

**Goal:** Go from zero customers to 5–10 paying customers ($500–1,000 MRR) in 7 days, executed in parallel with the product/deployment build.

This playbook requires no code — start executing today.

---

## Day 1: Foundation Setup (2–3 hours)

### 1. Build Your Target List (50 Plumbers/Electricians)

Use Google Maps + Facebook to find plumbing/electrical businesses in your target city (Melbourne recommended):

**Google Maps Search:**
- Search: "plumbing Melbourne" or "electrician Melbourne"
- Export list: business name, phone, address, website, review count
- Filter: 1–20 person businesses (your target), 4+ star reviews (professional)
- Goal: 30–40 names

**Facebook Strategy:**
- Join 10 trade groups: "Plumbers Melbourne", "Electricians Victoria", "Small Business Tradies Australia", etc.
- Find 20–30 active business owners (have profile photo, active in group)
- Collect: Facebook URL, business name

**LinkedIn:**
- Search: "plumbing business owner Melbourne" + "electrician owner Victoria"
- Filter: 1–20 employees, industries = construction/trades
- Collect: 10–15 names + LinkedIn profiles

**Result:** 50–70 target prospects to reach out to. Track them using the [Metrics Tracking sheet](METRICS_TRACKING.md).

---

## Day 1–2: Cold Outreach Campaign

Use the templates in [OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md](OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md).

- **Facebook Messenger:** Send message to 20–30 business owners (personalize each one)
- **Email:** Find business email on website, send cold email to 20–30 more
- **Phone/SMS:** If confident, call 5–10 and pitch verbally (highest conversion)
- **LinkedIn:** Message 10–15 (lower open rates, but professional vibe)

**Tracking:** Spreadsheet — name, contact method, date sent, response, status.
**Goal:** 20–30 responses in 3 days, 5–10 trial signups.

## Day 7 Onward: Optimization & Scale

**Track daily:**
- Outreach sent (target: 50+)
- Responses received (target: 20+)
- Trial signups (target: 5–10)
- Conversions to paid (target: 1–3)
- Referrals generated
- Revenue this week ($100–500)

**Double down on what's working:**
- Which channel brought the most signups? (Facebook, email, Reddit, cold call?)
- Which message resonated? (Copy what works)
- Which demo angle converts best? (ROI, time-saving, or lead quality?)

**Cut what's not working:**
- If cold email has <10% response, pause it
- If a Facebook group isn't generating signups, move to the next group
- If the referral program has no takers, adjust the incentive
