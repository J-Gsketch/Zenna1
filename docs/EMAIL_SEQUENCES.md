# Zenna Email Sequences

Copy-paste ready email nurture sequence for free trial signups, from Day 0 sign-up through Day 7 conversion.

---

## Email 1 (Day 0 — Sign-Up)

```
Subject: Welcome to Zenna! Here's Your Setup Link

Hi [Name],

You're all set to test Zenna for free for 7 days.

Step 1: Set up your business profile (3 mins)
Step 2: Connect your Twilio number (we'll help)
Step 3: Receive your first test call (5 mins)

Link: [SETUP BUTTON]

Questions? Reply to this email.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Email 2 (Day 2 — Engagement Check)

```
Subject: How's Your First Call Going?

Hi [Name],

Just checking in — did you receive your first test call yet?

If you need help with setup, I'm here. Just reply to this email.

If you're loving it, awesome! When you're ready to go live, let me know.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Email 3 (Day 5 — Conversion Push)

```
Subject: [Name], Your Trial Ends in 2 Days

Hi [Name],

Your 7-day Zenna trial expires in 2 days.

If you've seen the value (more calls booked, less admin), here's how to
stay on. Pay as you go — pick your tier:

- Starter: $99/mo (up to 100 calls)
- Pro: $249/mo (up to 500 calls)
- Enterprise: $499/mo (unlimited)

Here's your personal discount link (5% off, just for you):
[STRIPE CHECKOUT]

Or if it's not right for you yet, reply to this email. I'd love to hear
why.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Email 4 (Day 7 — Win-Back)

```
Subject: Last Chance: Your Zenna Trial Expires Today

Hi [Name],

Your trial expires at midnight.

If you want to keep going, upgrade here:
[STRIPE CHECKOUT]

If you need help or have questions, reply. I read every email.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Sequence Notes

- Send Email 1 immediately on trial signup (triggered by onboarding flow).
- Send Email 2 on Day 2 to catch anyone who hasn't completed setup.
- Send Email 3 on Day 5 with a personalized Stripe checkout discount link.
- Send Email 4 on Day 7 (trial expiry day) as a final conversion push.
- Track open/response rates in [METRICS_TRACKING.md](METRICS_TRACKING.md) and
  double down on whichever subject lines/CTAs convert best.
