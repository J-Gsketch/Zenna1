# Landing Page Messaging & Value Proposition — Zenna by Hammer & Code

## Core Value Proposition

> **"Never miss a lead. Stay on the tools."**

Zenna by Hammer & Code is an autonomous AI receptionist engineered for tradies, plumbing, electrical, and service operations. She catches missed calls while you work, qualifies customer suburban location & diagnostic call-out fees, collects Stripe deposits, and delivers a 6 PM daily briefing directly to your phone.

---

## Key Pillars

1. **Speed to Lead**: Missed calls automatically trigger a personalized SMS in under 3 seconds.
2. **The Tyre-Kicker Filter**: Enforces standard call-out diagnostic fees upfront before taking calendar slots.
3. **Multi-Tenant Setup**: 1-click self-serve setup with dual-currency AUD / NZD / USD support.
4. **Stripe & Google Integration**: Instant deposit invoices and Google Calendar/Drive sync.
