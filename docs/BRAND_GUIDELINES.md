# Brand Guidelines — Zenna by Hammer & Code

- **Official Product Name**: **Zenna by Hammer & Code**
- **Short Name**: Zenna
- **Brand Partnership Tagline**: "AI Receptionist for Tradies & Service Businesses by Hammer & Code"

## 2. Visual Palette & Styling
- **Primary Ink / Background**: `#0A1F3C` (Deep Navy Ink)
- **Accent Gold / Highlight**: `#FF6A1A` / `#D4AF37` (Hi-Vis Orange & Metallic Gold)
- **Text Paper**: `#F8FAFC` (Pure Light Paper)
- **Muted Subtext**: `#94A3B8` (Slate Muted)

- **Personality**: Direct, humble, professional Australian & New Zealand trade style.
- **Key Colloquialisms**: "G'day", "sorted", "no worries", "too easy", "on the tools".
- **Rule**: Maintain respectful professionalism while protecting operator hours from price-shoppers.
