# 12-Month Roadmap (Recommendations)

| Phase | Timeline | Focus | Target |
|-------|----------|-------|--------|
| **Launch** | Months 1–2 | Product MVP, founder visibility, first customers via direct outreach | 10 customers, $1K MRR |
| **Traction** | Months 3–4 | Content engine, partnerships, referrals, add electricians | 50 customers, $12K MRR |
| **Scale** | Months 5–6 | Geographic expansion (NZ), white-label conversations, paid integrations | 200+ customers, $50K MRR |
| **Dominate** | Months 7–9 | Multi-trade (HVAC), affiliate program, PR/speaking circuit | 500+ customers, $120K MRR |
| **Expansion** | Months 10–12 | Adjacent product launches (job mgmt, invoicing), Series A optionality | 1000+ customers, $250K MRR |

---

## Phase Detail

### Phase 1 — Launch (Months 1–2)
- Ship MVP with dynamic (per-tenant) AI prompts and Stripe billing.
- Founder posts 3–5x/week; 1 podcast guest spot.
- Direct outreach: 50 tradies/week via Facebook groups, LinkedIn, Google Maps scraping.
- 7-day free trial, no card. Concierge onboarding for every signup.
- **Gate to Phase 2:** 10 paying customers, 1 video testimonial, churn < 10%.

### Phase 2 — Traction (Months 3–4)
- Content engine: 2–3 blog posts/week targeting "how plumbers get more leads".
- Referral program live: $50 credit per converted referral.
- Pitch 3 incumbents (JobTracker, MYOB, Housecall Pro) for integration partnerships.
- Expand ICP: plumbers + electricians.
- Consider freemium tier if trial conversion is weak.
- **Gate to Phase 3:** 50 customers, 2 partnerships signed, organic traffic > 2K/mo.

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 5–6)
- NZ launch (NZD billing already supported in onboarding).
- White-label pitch to 1–2 incumbents (30/70 revenue share).
- Premium integrations ($50/mo add-ons).
- Hire first SDR or VA for outbound.
- **Gate to Phase 4:** 200 customers, CAC < $40, LTV:CAC > 5.

### Phase 4 — Dominate (Months 7–9)
- HVAC/Air Con trade launch.
- Affiliate program: 25–30% first-year commission, creator recruitment.
- PR push: trade publications, conference talks, award entries.
- **Gate to Phase 5:** 500 customers, NPS > 50, net revenue retention > 100%.

### Phase 5 — Expansion (Months 10–12)
- Adjacent products: job management lite, invoicing, review capture.
- Enterprise tier with custom integrations ($499+, white-label).
- Optional: raise Series A on $250K MRR traction, or stay profitable and compound.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm (all phases)

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Mon | Metrics review: MRR, churn, CAC, trial conversion |
| Tue | Content: 1 blog post + 1 video published |
| Wed | Customer calls: 3–5 check-ins with active users |
| Thu | Outreach: 20 new cold messages + partnership follow-ups |
| Fri | Ship: 1 product improvement based on the week's feedback |
