# Customer Success Materials — Zenna by Hammer & Code

Guide to driving long-term retention and customer satisfaction for **Zenna by Hammer & Code** partner tradies.

---

## 1. Executive Briefings (Daily at 6 PM)
Every evening at 6:00 PM, Zenna sends an SMS summary directly to the trade business owner:
- Total missed calls caught today
- Total new leads qualified
- Confirmed pipeline job value ($)
- Direct link to live dashboard

## 2. Customer Retention Milestones
- **Day 1**: Call forwarding verified via mobile test call (`*21*...#`).
- **Day 3**: First 10 automated SMS text-backs delivered.
- **Day 7**: First Stripe call-out fee deposit collected.
- **Day 30**: ROI review showing saved call time (avg. 12+ hours saved/mo).
