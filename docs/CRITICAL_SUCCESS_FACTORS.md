# Critical Success Factors

The 7 things that move the needle — distilled from the competitive analysis.

---

## 1. Founder Visibility
You must be the face of Zenna (like ServiceTitan's founder). Tradies buy from people they know and trust. Post weekly, speak at trade events, do the podcast circuit. Authenticity is a moat competitors cannot copy.

**Measure:** 3–5 posts/week, 1 podcast/month, 1 event/quarter.

## 2. Content Strategy
Organic traffic beats paid ads long-term (the HubSpot playbook). Rank for "how plumbers get more leads" and own the tradie-growth conversation.

**Measure:** 15 posts + 8 videos by Month 2; 5K organic visits/mo by Month 4.

## 3. Partnership First
Don't build everything — integrate with existing players (Stripe/Twilio playbook). JobTracker, MYOB, Xero and Housecall Pro already have your customers.

**Measure:** 3 incumbents pitched by Month 1; first integration live by Month 4.

## 4. Compliance Foundation
Build trust from day 1 — the Australian market expects it. ABN verification, GST handling, licensing checks.

**Measure:** ABN lookup live Month 1; tax compliance Month 2.

## 5. Product-Led Growth
Let the product sell itself (Calendly/Zapier playbook). Free trial with no credit card, instant "aha" (first simulated call booked in the dashboard), and **concierge onboarding** so no tradie ever touches technical setup.

**Measure:** trial→paid conversion > 20%; onboarding time < 24h (concierge) with < 10 min of customer effort.

## 6. Vertical Focus
Plumbers first, then electricians, then HVAC — sequentially (ServiceTitan playbook). Each trade has unique workflows; focus delivers a better product and natural cross-referrals.

**Measure:** plumbers-only until 10 paying customers and < 10% churn.

## 7. Pricing Confidence
$99–499/mo is the proven range for trade software. Never race to the bottom — one recovered job pays for months of Zenna. Sell ROI, not features.

**Measure:** zero discounted accounts below $99; ROI framing in 100% of sales conversations.

---

## And 1 Bonus: White-Label Ready

Be embeddable, not standalone (Twilio/Stripe playbook). The endgame distribution channel is other platforms reselling Zenna under their brand.

**Measure:** white-label term sheet by Month 6+.

---

## The One-Line Version

> Be visible, publish weekly, partner instead of competing, build compliance in, let the product sell with concierge onboarding, win plumbers before expanding, and never apologise for $99/mo — one saved job pays for it.
