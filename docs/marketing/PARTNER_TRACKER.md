## Outreach Cadence & Targets

- **Weekly:** 10 researched, 5–10 personalised emails sent, 3-touch follow-up over 2 weeks.
- **Monthly:** 8–12 new conversations; Tier 3 (government) applications always in progress.
- **Quarterly:** 1–2 signed agreements minimum; Tier 1 integration partners prioritised Q1–Q2, white-label (Tier 5) prioritised Q3–Q4.

## Standard Terms Library

| Deal type | Standard terms |
|---|---|
| Integration partner | Free mutual integration; co-marketing announcement; marketplace listing |
| Association member perk | 20% member discount; 10% affiliate to the association |
| Coach/creator affiliate | 20% recurring for 12 months (or $50/customer one-off for small creators) |
| Media/podcast | Guest spot first; sponsor only after audience data verified |
| Government/association listing | Free — the work is the application, not the fee |
| White-label | 60/40 revenue share (partner keeps 40%); min. 20 seats |
