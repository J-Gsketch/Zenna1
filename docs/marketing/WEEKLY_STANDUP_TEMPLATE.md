# Weekly Standup Template — Friday 10:00, 30–45 min

> The founder's single recurring marketing meeting. Agent prepares the metrics pack in advance; the meeting is decisions, not status recitation.

## Attendees
- Founder (strategy, approvals, decisions)
- Marketing agent (execution, metrics, recommendations)

## Agenda

### 1. Wins This Week (5 min)
What worked — with numbers:
- Blog post ranking?
- Cold email that converted?
- Community post that went viral?
- Customer story that's compelling?

### 2. Metrics Review (10 min)
Trend check against [METRICS_DASHBOARD.md](./METRICS_DASHBOARD.md) weekly KPIs:
- New signups (trial + free)
- Trial → paid conversions
- Organic traffic (GA4)
- CAC by channel
- Churn (cancellations)

### 3. Blockers (5 min)
What's in the way:
- Content calendar backing up?
- Partners slow to respond?
- Paid ads underperforming?
- Metrics unclear or untracked?

### 4. Next Week Plan (10 min)
- Content deadlines
- Campaign launches
- Partner milestones
- Paid ads adjustments

### 5. Approvals (5–10 min, batched)
- Founder-voice content (LinkedIn posts, bylines) — approve async during the week, confirm here
- Founder appearance requests (podcast, speaking) — accept / delegate / decline
- Spend changes beyond agent's standing authority (>$500 new line items)

## Standing Rules

1. **Metrics pack before the meeting.** If the dashboard isn't updated, the standup is a 5-minute reschedule, not a data-entry session.
2. **Decisions get logged.** If it's not in the decision log, it wasn't decided.
3. **One meeting.** Everything else is async. The founder's marketing time commitment is this meeting + batched approvals (~1.5 hrs/week total).
4. **Red flags have owners.** Every metric in the red leaves the room with a fix, a pause, or a pivot — never "watch it".
