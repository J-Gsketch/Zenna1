# Weekly Marketing Tasks — Agent + Founder

> Weekly rhythm that keeps the machine accountable. The agent executes; the founder's only recurring commitments are the **Friday check-in (1 hr)** and **async content approvals (~30 min)**. Everything customer-facing is handled by the agent and automation.

## Weekly Planning (2 hrs, Tuesday morning — agent)

- Review previous week's metrics against targets.
- Prioritise next week's campaigns.
- Identify top performer (organic win, great customer story, viral post).
- Adjust strategy if a channel is consistently off-target.

## Weekly Content Calendar (agent maintains)

- 2 blog posts scheduled
- 1 video script ready (recording scheduled)
- 3 social media posts queued (Buffer)
- 2 email sequences active / in optimisation
- 5 cold emails or partner outreach messages scheduled

Template: [CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md](./CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md)

## Weekly Customer Win Capture (agent, 1 hr)

- Identify 1 customer with a great story (from usage data / NPS responses — not founder conversations).
- Request a short testimonial via automated email (text or video).
- Start a case study interview (async questionnaire is fine).
- Produce one social proof asset: quote graphic, video clip, or case study page.

## Weekly Founder Check-In (1 hr, Friday)

Founder's single recurring meeting. Agenda per [WEEKLY_STANDUP_TEMPLATE.md](./WEEKLY_STANDUP_TEMPLATE.md):

- Review metrics together.
- Celebrate wins.
- Discuss pivots if a channel misses target 2+ weeks running.
- Approve batched content (LinkedIn posts written in founder voice, case studies).
- Approve any founder appearances the agent has lined up (podcast, speaking gig). Founder chooses whether to appear or delegate.

## Month-End Review (3 hrs, agent prepares; founder reviews)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Total new customers | Phase target ([QUARTERLY_PHASES.md](./QUARTERLY_PHASES.md)) |
| MRR growth | 10–15% month-over-month |
| CAC | <$50 blended |
| LTV | Calculate and trend (target LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1) |
| Churn | <3% monthly (trending to <2%) |
| NPS | 65+ (trending to 70+) |
| Organic traffic | 30% MoM growth |
| Top channel | Double down next month |
| Worst channel | Pause or pivot |

### Partner Updates (monthly)
- Follow up active partnerships; celebrate shared wins.
- Negotiate next-tier agreements where traction exists.
- Onboard newly signed partners (integration or co-marketing).

### Content Audit (monthly)
- Promote top-performing posts again (social, newsletter, ads).
- Update or rewrite underperformers.
- Log new keyword opportunities and backlink targets.

### Paid Ads Review (monthly)
- Ad spend vs. revenue; ROAS target 5:1+.
- Rotate winning creative; retire losers.
- Review audience insights — who converts best?

## Weekly Definition of Done

- [ ] Tuesday planning session complete; calendar updated
- [ ] All content produced and scheduled per calendar
- [ ] 1 customer win captured and 1 social proof asset shipped
- [ ] Friday standup held; decision log and action items recorded
- [ ] Weekly metrics report filed in the dashboard
- [ ] (Month-end weeks) Monthly review pack prepared for founder
