# Quarterly Phases — Q1–Q4 Targets & Execution Plans

> The 12-month roadmap broken into four phase gates. Each quarter has a hard target, a focus, and defined weekly output. Missing a phase gate twice in a row triggers a strategy pivot review with the founder.

## Phase Overview

| Quarter | Phase | Customers | MRR (subs) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (M1–3) | Launch | 50 | $5K | Product MVP + brand visibility |
| Q2 (M4–6) | Traction | 200+ | $50K | Content compounding, partnerships live, referrals accelerating |
| Q3 (M7–9) | Scaling | 500+ | $120K | Geographic expansion, white-label, affiliate program |
| Q4 (M10–12) | Dominance | 1,000+ | $250K | Adjacent products, Series A prep, market consolidation |

---

## Q1 — Launch (Months 1–3)

**Target:** 50 customers, $5K MRR
**Focus:** Prove the message-market fit. Foundational SEO content, first integrations attached, brand visibility established.

**Weekly output:**
- 8–10 blog posts/month (foundational SEO content)
- 4 videos/month (product demo, customer story)
- 2–3 podcast appearances/quarter (agent books; founder or agent-recorded)
- 100+ cold outreach emails/quarter (partnerships)
- 2–3 speaking engagements/quarter (trade events — agent pitches, founder optionally appears)

**Key milestones:**
- [ ] Email domain authentication complete (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) before first campaign
- [ ] First 10 customers via direct/community channels
- [ ] First integration partner signed (target: Xero or ServiceM8)
- [ ] Referral program live ($50/customer credit)
- [ ] 4 case studies published
- [ ] Government channel listing submitted (business.gov.au directory, relevant state small-business program)

**Gate to Q2:** 50 paying customers AND <$60 blended CAC AND churn <5%.

---

## Q2 — Traction (Months 4–6)

**Target:** 200+ customers, $50K MRR
**Focus:** Content compounds, partnerships launch, referral flywheel spins up.

**Weekly output:**
- 12–15 blog posts/month (increased volume; guest posts from partners)
- 8 videos/month (YouTube channel building momentum)
- 4 podcast appearances/quarter (industry recognition growing)
- 5–10 partner launches/quarter (integrations going live)
- 50+ referrals/week by end of quarter (organic + incentivised)

**Key milestones:**
- [ ] 3+ integrations live in partner marketplaces (Xero App Store, MYOB, ServiceM8)
- [ ] Affiliate/creator program launched
- [ ] YouTube channel: 1K+ subscribers
- [ ] Organic traffic: 10K visits/month
- [ ] NPS baseline established (65+)
- [ ] Second marketing hire evaluated (content freelancer → part-time agent)

**Gate to Q3:** 200 customers AND 15% MoM MRR growth sustained for 2+ months AND LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1.

---

## Q3 — Scaling (Months 7–9)

**Target:** 500+ customers, $120K MRR
**Focus:** Geographic expansion (NZ first), white-label offering, affiliate program at scale.

**Weekly output:**
- 15–20 blog posts/month (content moat + SEO dominance)
- 10 videos/month (YouTube channel 10K+ subs by end of quarter)
- 6+ podcast appearances/quarter (thought-leader status)
- 10+ white-label partnerships signed (passive revenue)
- 100+ affiliate signups (creator program growing)

**Key milestones:**
- [ ] NZ launch (localised landing pages, NZ trade groups, NZD pricing)
- [ ] White-label product live (agencies, franchises, multi-trade groups)
- [ ] $10K+ MRR from integrations + white-label combined
- [ ] Organic traffic: 25K visits/month
- [ ] Marketing team: 2 agents (or 1 agent + dedicated content team)
- [ ] Government/association channel producing steady referrals (e.g. listed in 2+ trade association resource hubs)

**Gate to Q4:** 500 customers AND churn <2.5% AND white-label pipeline ≥ 10 active deals.

---

## Q4 — Dominance (Months 10–12)

**Target:** 1,000+ customers, $250K MRR (+$50K integrations/white-label = $300K total)
**Focus:** Adjacent product launches, Series A prep, market consolidation.

**Weekly output:**
- 20+ blog posts/month (content platform; 50K visits/month)
- 12 videos/month (YouTube: 50K+ subs)
- 8+ podcast appearances/quarter (category-celebrity status in trade space)
- 20+ active partnerships (ecosystem becomes the moat)
- 500+ monthly referrals (network effects)

**Key milestones:**
- [ ] $300K total MRR
- [ ] Adjacent product scoped (Zenna product #2 under Hammer & Code)
- [ ] Series A data room ready (metrics, cohorts, unit economics)
- [ ] Category leadership: default answer to "AI receptionist for tradies" in AU
- [ ] NPS 70+, churn <2%

## Pivot Triggers (any quarter)

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| MRR growth <8% MoM for 2 months | Founder + agent strategy reset; reallocate channel budget |
| CAC >$80 blended for 1 month | Pause worst paid channel; shift spend to top organic channel |
| Churn >4% for 1 month | Product problem, not marketing — escalate to product roadmap |
| Partnership pipeline <3 active conversations | Double outreach volume; revisit partner target list |
