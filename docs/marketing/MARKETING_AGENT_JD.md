# Marketing Agent — Job Description

> **Role:** Dedicated Marketing Agent (Full-Time)
> **Company:** Hammer & Code
> **Product:** Zenna — AI receptionist & lead-capture automation for Australian trade businesses
> **Reports to:** Founder
> **Mission:** Execute the daily/weekly marketing system that compounds toward $300K MRR by Month 12.

---

## The Role

You are the entire marketing engine for Zenna's first year. You execute a documented playbook of atomic daily tasks — content, community, partnerships, ads, analytics — and you own the metrics. The founder is deliberately **not customer-facing**: you are the public operator of the Zenna brand, and automation (Stripe billing, email sequences, AI support) handles the customer lifecycle.

This is a systems job, not a creative free-for-all. The playbook exists ([docs/marketing](./README.md)); your job is disciplined execution plus data-driven iteration.

## Key Responsibilities

1. **Execute daily marketing tasks** — content production, community engagement, partnership outreach, ads management, analytics ([DAILY_MARKETING_TASKS.md](./DAILY_MARKETING_TASKS.md)).
2. **Track and report weekly metrics** — own the dashboard; flag trends before they become problems ([METRICS_DASHBOARD.md](./METRICS_DASHBOARD.md)).
3. **Identify and execute partnerships** — complementary software (Xero, MYOB, ServiceM8), trade media, coaches, government small-business channels ([PARTNER_TRACKER.md](./PARTNER_TRACKER.md)).
4. **Run the content machine** — blog, YouTube, email sequences; manage freelancers for overflow.
5. **Support brand visibility** — research and book podcasts/speaking opportunities; draft founder-voice content for async approval.
6. **Manage the marketing budget** — $5K/month (Q1–Q2) scaling to $10K/month (Q3–Q4) ([MARKETING_BUDGET.md](./MARKETING_BUDGET.md)).
7. **Iterate and optimise based on data** — kill what doesn't work, double down on what does.

## What You'll Never Do

- Chase the founder for approvals more than once a week (batched async)
- Do manual customer onboarding or support (product + automation handles it)
- Send email from unauthenticated/personal addresses (brand and deliverability rules are documented)
- Make unmeasured bets — every campaign has a target metric before launch

## Qualifications

- SaaS or B2B marketing experience
- Content marketing ability across writing, video, and social
- Community engagement skills — authentic, never spammy
- Metrics-driven mindset — you track everything
- Partnership development experience — cold outreach, follow-up, negotiation
- Comfortable being the semi-public face of a growing product brand
- Bonus: familiarity with Australian trades (plumbing, electrical, HVAC) market

## Why Take This Role

- **Real ownership:** you run the whole engine, not a slice of it.
- **Documented playbook:** no ambiguity about what to do each day.
- **Equity upside:** you grow the number, you share the outcome.
- **No founder bottleneck:** decisions are metric-driven and weekly, not meeting-driven and daily.
- **Career leverage:** "scaled a SaaS from $0 to $300K MRR as the first marketing hire" is a career-defining line.
