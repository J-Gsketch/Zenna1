> **Company:** Hammer & Code (parent company)
> **Product:** Zenna (product #1 — AI receptionist & lead-capture automation for Australian tradies)
> **Grand Task:** Scale Zenna to **$300K MRR** by Month 12 through a dedicated marketing agent executing atomic daily tasks.

## Operating Principles

1. **Autonomous by design.** The marketing agent + automation stack runs the machine. The founder commits real strategic time (weekly review, quarterly planning, budget approvals) but has **zero customer-facing involvement** — no sales calls, no support, no onboarding sessions. The agent is the operator; the founder is the owner.
2. **Atomic tasks compound.** Every day has a defined task list (see [DAILY_MARKETING_TASKS.md](./DAILY_MARKETING_TASKS.md)). Small, repeatable actions compound into content libraries, partner networks, and referral flywheels.
3. **Everything is measured.** No decision on gut feel. Every channel ties to a metric in [METRICS_DASHBOARD.md](./METRICS_DASHBOARD.md).
4. **Attach, don't build.** Zenna grows faster by attaching to prebuilt complementary software (Xero, MYOB, ServiceM8, Jobber, Fergus) and trusted channels (trade associations, government small-business programs) than by building everything from scratch. See [PARTNER_TRACKER.md](./PARTNER_TRACKER.md).
5. **Brand architecture is fixed.** All marketing ships under the **Zenna** product brand, with **Hammer & Code** as the parent/company entity (used for corporate, hiring, legal, and future products). See the brand rules in [MARKETING_TOOLS_STACK.md](./MARKETING_TOOLS_STACK.md#brand--email-identity).

## Accountability Loop

- **Daily:** agent executes task list, logs metrics (30 min/day).
- **Weekly:** Friday standup vs. KPI targets ([WEEKLY_STANDUP_TEMPLATE.md](./WEEKLY_STANDUP_TEMPLATE.md)).
- **Monthly:** milestone review — customers, MRR, CAC, LTV, churn, NPS, traffic.
- **Quarterly:** phase gate — hit the phase targets in [QUARTERLY_PHASES.md](./QUARTERLY_PHASES.md) or pivot strategy before the next quarter begins.

## Success Indicators (System Working)

- **Consistency:** same tasks executed daily/weekly/monthly (discipline over intensity)
- **Metrics:** dashboard shows improving trends (data-driven)
- **Compounding:** content ranking, referrals growing, organic accelerating
- **Partnership wins:** 2–3 partnerships launching per quarter
- **Customer stories:** 4+ case studies per month
- **Founder/brand recognition:** 4+ podcast appearances per quarter (agent-booked, optionally founder-recorded or ghost-written)
- **MRR growth:** 15% month-over-month — the ultimate metric

## Why This Works

1. **Atomic tasks** — the agent knows exactly what to do each day; no ambiguity.
2. **Compound growth** — small daily tasks add up to massive results over 12 months.
3. **Accountability** — clear metrics, weekly check-ins, visible progress.
4. **Scalability** — a second agent can be hired without friction; the playbook is documented.
5. **Founder leverage** — the founder focuses on strategy and product; the agent executes marketing; automation handles customers.
6. **Data-driven** — every decision ties to metrics, not gut feel.
7. **Repeatable** — the same system works in Month 1 and Month 12; only the volumes scale.
