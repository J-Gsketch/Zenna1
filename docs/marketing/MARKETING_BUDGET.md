## ROI Targets

| Metric | Target | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Blended CAC | <$50 | Weekly |
| Paid ads ROAS | ≥ 5:1 | Monthly |
| LTV:CAC ratio | ≥ 3:1 | Monthly |
| Cost per organic signup | Trending down MoM | Monthly |
| Content cost per ranking keyword | <$200 (freelance) | Quarterly |

## Spend Rules (agent-enforced)

1. **Paid ads never scale before CAC proof.** A channel earns +50% budget only after 2 consecutive weeks under CAC target.
2. **Kill fast.** Any ad set >$50 cost per trial for 1 week is paused, no exceptions.
3. **Freelance before headcount.** Overflow work goes to freelancers until Q3; second agent hire only after the Q2 gate is passed.
4. **Commissions don't count against CAC targets twice.** Referral-acquired customers are tracked as their own channel.
5. **Every line item maps to a metric.** If a spend can't be tied to a number in [METRICS_DASHBOARD.md](./METRICS_DASHBOARD.md), it doesn't get spent.

## Payback Maths (sanity check)

- ARPU: ~$250/month → $50 CAC pays back in **<1 week of subscription revenue**.
- At 2% monthly churn, average customer lifetime ≈ 50 months → LTV ≈ $12,500 (gross).
- Even at 5% churn (early days), LTV ≈ $5,000 → LTV:CAC = 100:1 at target CAC. The constraint is growth speed, not unit economics.
