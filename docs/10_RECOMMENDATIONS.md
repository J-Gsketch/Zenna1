# 10 Recommendations for Zenna

Actionable plays adapted from Calendly, Twilio, Stripe, ServiceTitan, HubSpot and Zapier.

---

## 1. Position as "Lead Multiplier," Not "Receptionist"

**Why:** Receptionist = commodity; lead multiplier = strategic tool.

**What to do:**
- Change messaging: "Book 3X More Jobs Without Hiring."
- Feature storytelling: "Dave booked 15 jobs/week with Zenna, was doing 8 before."
- Pricing narrative: "$99/mo tool that books $1000+/week in jobs."
- Marketing: show ROI, not features.

**Why it works:** Calendly didn't sell "scheduling software," they sold "get your time back." ServiceTitan doesn't sell "software," they sell "eliminate paperwork." HubSpot doesn't sell "CRM," they sell "grow faster without hiring."

---

## 2. Launch Freemium Tier (Not Just Free Trial)

**Why:** HubSpot's freemium got 5M free users, converting at 3–5%. Better long-term than trial-only.

**What to do:**
- Free: 10 calls/month + basic booking.
- Starter: $99/mo, 100 calls/month.
- Pro: $249/mo, 500 calls/month.
- Enterprise: $499/mo, unlimited.

**Why it works:** low-friction entry, longer evaluation, product-led growth, network effects.

**Risk:** free tier attracts tire-kickers. **Mitigation:** after 3 months on free, "upgrade or we pause your account."

---

## 3. Invest in Content Marketing (Not Just Ads)

**Why:** HubSpot's blog gets 15M+ views/month. Organic beats paid long-term.

**What to do:**
- Blog: 2–3 posts/week (freelancer @ ~$100/post).
- Target keywords: "how plumbers get more leads", "best call system for trades".
- YouTube: 1–2 videos/week (demo + case study format).
- Podcast: "Tradies Scaling Faster" (interview successful customers).
- Guest posts: Jora, Seek, trade publications.

**6-month timeline:**
- Months 1–2: 15 blog posts, 8 videos (establish authority).
- Months 3–4: ranking for 10–15 keywords, ~5K/month organic traffic.
- Months 5–6: ~15K/month organic traffic, 2–3 customers/week from organic.

**ROI:** ~$400/month cost → 200 customers over 6 months → ~$50K MRR.

---

## 4. Partner With Incumbents (Don't Compete)

**Why:** Twilio didn't build phone systems; they partnered. Stripe didn't build e-commerce; they powered it.

**What to do:**
- Approach JobTracker: "We integrate with your platform, send you customers."
- Approach MYOB/Xero: "Lead capture → your invoicing, better workflow."
- Approach Housecall Pro: "Use Zenna for calls, sync to Housecall for job management."
- Revenue share: 10–20% of customer subscription (win-win).

**Timeline:** Month 1 pitch 3 incumbents → Months 2–3 negotiate → Month 4+ live integration and co-marketing.

---

## 5. Double Down on Founder-Led Sales & Community

**Why:** ServiceTitan's founder is visible, trusted, famous in the trade space. That's a moat.

**What to do:**
- Be the face of Zenna (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, podcasts).
- Post weekly: tradie wins, business tips, product updates.
- Speak at trade events (plumbing conferences, business associations).
- Monthly calls with top 10 customers.
- Guest on podcasts: "How I Built Zenna for Aussie Tradies."

**Timeline:** Month 1 LinkedIn active + 1 podcast → Months 2–3 speak at 1–2 events → Month 4+ monthly customer calls + weekly content.

---

## 6. Build for Compliance From Day 1

**Why:** Australian regulations are strict; tradies expect compliance verification.

**What to do:**
- ABN verification (ABR API): check every customer is legitimate.
- Tax compliance: auto-calculate GST on invoices.
- SafeWork notifications: remind customers of safety requirements.
- Licensing registry checks: electricians/gas fitters must be licensed.
- Insurance recommendations for high-risk services.

**Timeline:** Month 1 ABN lookup live → Month 2 tax compliance → Month 3+ licensing/insurance integrations.

---

## 7. Expand to Adjacent Trades Sequentially

**Why:** ServiceTitan started with HVAC, then expanded. Focused > scattered.

**Phases:**
- Phase 1 (Months 1–3): Plumbers only.
- Phase 2 (Months 4–6): + Electricians.
- Phase 3 (Months 7–9): + HVAC/Air Con.
- Phase 4 (Months 10–12): + Builders, Locksmiths, etc.

**Why it works:** each trade has unique workflows; cross-sell happens naturally (plumber refers electrician); market size multiplies each phase.

---

## 8. Make White-Label a Revenue Stream

**Why:** Stripe, Twilio and Zapier all benefit from being embedded in other platforms.

**What to do:**
- License Zenna to JobTracker (resold as "JobTracker Receptionist").
- Revenue: 30% of Zenna subscription fee (partner keeps 70%).
- White-label: partner branding, Zenna backend.
- Goal: partner customers auto-upgrade to the bundle.

**Timeline:** Month 6+ (after proving Zenna works standalone).

---

## 9. Build Paid Integrations as Future Revenue

**Why:** HubSpot's marketplace has 1000+ integrations, many paid.

**What to do:**
- Zapier integration: free, let users create any workflow.
- Premium integrations: Housecall Pro → Zenna ($50/mo add-on).
- Custom integrations for enterprise ($200+/mo).

**Why it works:** low dev cost, high margin, raises LTV ($99 → $149+).

**Timeline:** Months 4–6, after MVP is stable.

---

## 10. Build a Creator/Affiliate Program

**Why:** Zapier and Calendly grew via creators and affiliates recommending them.

**What to do:**
- Commission: 25–30% of first-year subscription.
- Targets: YouTube creators, business coaches, accountants serving tradies.
- Provide: demo videos, marketing materials, demo accounts.
- Payout: monthly via Stripe.

**Why it works:** creators have built-in trust, you only pay on conversion, and there's a viral loop (creators promoting Zenna attract more creators).

**Timeline:** Month 2+ (set up affiliate tracking).

---

## Bonus: Concierge Onboarding (from founder feedback)

Tradies won't self-serve a 30-minute technical setup. Add a **done-for-you onboarding service**: customer answers 5 questions (10 minutes), the Zenna team configures everything within 24 hours. This is the "elaborate but not complicate" answer — the *product* is sophisticated, the *customer experience* is a phone call. See [CONCIERGE_ONBOARDING.md](./CONCIERGE_ONBOARDING.md).
