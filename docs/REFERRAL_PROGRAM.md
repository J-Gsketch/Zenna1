# Zenna Referral Program

Launch a simple, viral referral incentive to turn happy customers into a
customer acquisition channel.

---

## Offer

**$50 credit for every successful referral.**

- Existing customer refers a new tradie business.
- When the referred business becomes a paying customer (completes their
  first paid month), the referring customer receives a $50 account credit
  (or cash payout, founder's choice).
- The new customer also gets 5% off their first month as a welcome
  incentive.

## How to Launch

1. Announce the program via email to all active trial/paying customers
   (use the template below).
2. Give each customer a unique referral link or code (can be as simple as
   `?ref=[customer-id]` appended to the signup link until a formal referral
   system is built).
3. Track referrals manually in [METRICS_TRACKING.md](METRICS_TRACKING.md)
   until volume justifies automation.

## Announcement Email Template

```
Subject: Get $50 for Every Tradie You Refer to Zenna

Hi [Name],

Know another plumber or electrician who's missing calls while on the
tools? Refer them to Zenna and get $50 credited to your account once
they become a paying customer.

Here's your referral link: [LINK]

They'll get 5% off their first month, and you'll get $50 credit. Win-win.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
```

## Program Rules

- Referral must be a new customer (not an existing trial or past
  customer).
- Credit is issued once the referred customer completes their first paid
  billing cycle.
- No limit on the number of referrals per customer.

## Tracking & Payout

- Maintain a simple referral log: referrer, referred business, date
  referred, date converted, credit issued (Y/N).
- Review weekly alongside [METRICS_TRACKING.md](METRICS_TRACKING.md) to see
  how much of weekly growth is coming from referrals.

## Optimization

- If referrals stall, consider increasing the incentive or adding a
  double-sided reward tier for referrers who bring in 3+ customers.
- Promote the program in email nurture sequences (see
  [EMAIL_SEQUENCES.md](EMAIL_SEQUENCES.md)) and community engagement posts
  (see [COMMUNITY_ENGAGEMENT.md](COMMUNITY_ENGAGEMENT.md)).
