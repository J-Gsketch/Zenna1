# Founder Playbook: Becoming the Tradie-Software Authority

ServiceTitan's founder became famous in the trade space — that visibility is a moat competitors can't copy. This playbook is how Zenna's founder builds the same position.

---

## Core Positioning

**You are not a tech founder selling to tradies. You are the person who fixes the missed-call problem for tradies.**

- Story: "Every tradie I know loses jobs because they're under a house when the phone rings. I built Zenna so that never happens again."
- Enemy: missed calls, tyre-kickers, paperwork, voicemail.
- Hero: the solo tradie who wants to grow without hiring office staff.

## Weekly Content Cadence

| Day | Channel | Content |
|-----|---------|---------|
| Mon | LinkedIn | Tradie business tip (no product pitch) |
| Wed | LinkedIn + Facebook groups | Customer win or metric ("Dave booked 3 extra jobs this week") |
| Fri | LinkedIn | Product update / behind-the-scenes build |
| Weekly | Podcasts | 1 guest appearance or outreach to 5 hosts |
| Monthly | YouTube | 1 longer demo or customer story |

## Signature Talks (for trade events & podcasts)

1. **"The $50K Missed Call"** — how much revenue tradies lose to voicemail each year, with real numbers.
2. **"Book 3X More Jobs Without Hiring"** — the lead-multiplier framework.
3. **"AI Won't Replace Tradies, But Tradies Using AI Will Replace Those Who Don't"** — AI adoption talk for skeptical audiences.

## Speaking Circuit (AU/NZ)

- Plumbing Industry Association events
- Master Electricians / NECA chapter meetings
- Local business association breakfasts
- Trades-focused podcasts and YouTube channels
- Small-business expos (book a booth with live Zenna demos)

## Monthly Customer Ritual

- Call your top 10 customers personally, every month.
- Ask: "What almost made you cancel?" and "What would you tell a mate about Zenna?"
- Turn answers into testimonials, case studies and product fixes.
- Post (with permission) the best quotes — social proof compounds.

## Credibility Assets to Build

- [ ] 3 video testimonials (60–90s, iPhone quality is fine)
- [ ] 2 written case studies with hard metrics
- [ ] 1 "state of the trade" data report from Zenna call data (e.g., "Tradies miss 38% of calls during work hours")
- [ ] Media kit: bio, headshots, logo, one-pager
- [ ] Podcast one-sheet: your story + 3 talk topics

## Rules

1. **Be specific.** "Booked 15 jobs last week" beats "grow your business."
2. **Be a tradie advocate first, vendor second.** Give away the advice; sell the automation.
3. **Never fake it.** Tradies smell inauthenticity instantly. Real numbers, real customers, real accent.
4. **Show up weekly.** Authority is built by consistency, not virality.
