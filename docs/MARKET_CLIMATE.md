# Market Climate Analysis: Trade Software in 2024+

Seven macro trends that favor Zenna's timing in the Australian/NZ trade market.

---

## 1. AI Adoption in SMB
- ChatGPT adoption means tradies are comfortable with AI.
- COVID accelerated digital adoption across every trade.
- Younger tradies (Gen Z entering the trades) expect modern software.
- **Opportunity:** Zenna feels cutting-edge, not scary.

## 2. Labor Shortage in Trades
- Plumbers and electricians are slammed — more calls than capacity.
- Businesses need to handle more calls with the same staff.
- Traditional phone systems (landlines) are dying.
- **Opportunity:** Zenna = capacity multiplier (book more jobs without hiring).

## 3. Consolidation of Trade Software
- ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and JobTracker dominate the category.
- Smaller niche tools are being acquired.
- Platform integrations are winning over standalone tools.
- **Opportunity:** Partner with incumbents; don't compete head-to-head.

## 4. Payment Processing Maturity
- Stripe, Square and PayPal are dominant and trusted.
- Tradies expect to collect payment day-of-job.
- Deposit collection is now table stakes, not a feature.
- **Opportunity:** Zenna's deposit collection is baseline credibility, not the differentiator.

## 5. Remote Work Shift
- Tradies were always "remote" (on-site), but admin is increasingly remote (quote prep, invoicing).
- Mobile-first design is non-negotiable.
- **Opportunity:** Zenna dashboard accessible from phone, site, anywhere.

## 6. Government Compliance Tightening
- ABN verification (ATO cracking down on sham contracting).
- Insurance requirements increasing (clients want proof).
- Tax compliance automation is an expected feature.
- **Opportunity:** Zenna + ABN/compliance checks = full trust.

## 7. Subscription Model Maturity
- Tradies now expect recurring billing (not suspicious of it).
- Free trials are standard (no credit card required).
- Enterprise features = upsell norm.
- **Opportunity:** $99–499/mo pricing feels right-sized for the category.

---

## Net Read

The window is open: AI is normalized, tradies are capacity-constrained, and incumbents have educated the market on paying $99–499/mo for trade software. Zenna's wedge — answering missed calls and converting them into booked, deposit-paid jobs — sits directly on top of the labor-shortage pain, with none of the implementation weight of a full job-management suite.
