## Blog Calendar (Months 1–2)

**Week 1:** "How Much Missed Calls Actually Cost a Tradie (Real Numbers)" · "AI Receptionist for Plumbers: What It Is and What It Isn't"
**Week 2:** "How to Stop Missing Calls When You're Under a House" · "The 5-Minute Setup That Books Jobs While You Work"
**Week 3:** "Missed Call Text-Back in Australia: How It Works" · "Why Tyre-Kickers Call at 2pm (and How to Filter Them)"
**Week 4:** "Best Call Answering Options for Tradies in 2024 (Compared)" · "From Voicemail to Booked Job: A Plumber's Week With Zenna"
**Weeks 5–8:** Repeat cadence — 1 problem-aware + 1 solution/decision-stage post per week, plus 1 customer case study per fortnight.

## YouTube (1–2 videos/week)

**Formats:**
1. **Demo (3–5 min):** "Watch Zenna answer a real plumbing call" — screen recording + live call audio.
2. **Case study (5–8 min):** customer interview with hard numbers ("was doing 8 jobs/week, now 15").
3. **How-to (3 min):** "Set up call forwarding on Telstra in 60 seconds."

Shorts/Reels/TikTok: cut 30–45s highlights from every long video.

## Podcast

**"Tradies Scaling Faster"** — weekly 20-min interviews with successful trade business owners.
- Not about Zenna — about *their* growth stories. Zenna is the sponsor.
- Distribution: Spotify, Apple, YouTube, audiograms to social.

## Guest Posts & PR Targets

- Jora / Seek business blogs
- Australian trade publications (Plumbing Connection, Electrical Connection)
- Small business publications (SmartCompany, Kochie's Business Builders)
- Local news: "Local founder builds AI for tradies" angle

## Email Newsletter (weekly)

- List built from blog subscribers + trial signups.
- Content: 1 tradie growth tip + 1 customer win + 1 product note.
- Goal: nurture free/trial users to paid; keep churn low with wins.

## 6-Month Targets

| Milestone | Month |
|-----------|-------|
| 15 blog posts + 8 videos live | 2 |
| Ranking for 10–15 keywords, ~5K organic visits/mo | 4 |
| ~15K organic visits/mo, 2–3 customers/week from organic | 6 |

## Budget

- Freelance writer: ~$100/post × 3/week ≈ $1,200/mo (or founder-written for $0).
- Video: founder + iPhone + CapCut ≈ $0.
- Total: $0–1,500/mo depending on founder bandwidth.
