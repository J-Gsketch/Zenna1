## 1. Parties, purpose, and order of precedence

1.1 These terms are between the Zenna contracting entity named above (**Zenna**) and the contractor named above (**Contractor**).

1.2 The Contractor is engaged solely to support customer acquisition for Zenna’s small Route A pilot: a concierge missed-call text-back service for eligible New Zealand electrician/sparky businesses. The pilot is limited to the first five accepted customers.

1.3 The operative commercial offer for this engagement is **Zenna Route A — NZ Electrician Pilot Offer, Version 1.0** (the **Offer Source of Truth**). Every contractor briefing, prospect interaction, pipeline record, handoff, customer offer, activation record, and bounty decision must follow it. If these draft terms conflict with the Offer Source of Truth, the Offer Source of Truth prevails to the extent of the conflict.

1.4 The Contractor must use only the approved battle card and approved positioning. The approved 30-second positioning is:

> “Zenna is running a small missed-call text-back pilot for Kiwi electricians. If you are on a job and cannot answer, eligible calls can be conditionally forwarded to a Zenna number, which sends the caller a message asking for their name, suburb, and job details. It is not a receptionist or booking service, and availability is confirmed after a quick operations check and test. Would you be open to a short handoff with Zenna Operations to see whether it suits your current setup?”

## 2. Independent contractor status and no authority

2.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor, not Zenna’s employee, partner, agent, representative with authority, joint venturer, or customer employee. The Contractor controls the manner and timing of its permitted acquisition activity, subject to these terms and the Offer Source of Truth.

2.2 The Contractor has **no authority** to bind Zenna, make commitments for Zenna, vary any Zenna term, accept a customer, take payment, issue a refund, or make promises on Zenna’s behalf.

2.3 The Contractor must not represent themself as a customer’s employee or as Zenna’s founder personally.

2.4 Nothing in these terms guarantees the Contractor any minimum work, leads, income, term, territory, exclusivity, customer acceptance, or bounty.

## 3. Scope of permitted services

3.1 The Contractor may perform only the following acquisition activities:

- build prospect lists from permitted public sources;
- start a short, respectful conversation using the approved battle card;
- qualify likely fit and record the required facts in the Zenna pilot pipeline;
- capture business name, contact name, mobile, service area, current missed-call process, interest status, and **explicit consent** to a Zenna Operations handoff;
- keep accurate pipeline records; and
- arrange an Operations handoff.

3.2 The intended customer is a New Zealand electrician/sparky business with 1–3 vans, a working business mobile number, genuine inbound enquiry calls, and no dedicated receptionist. Best-fit customers are owner-operators frequently on the tools, driving, or unable to answer every new customer call.

3.3 The Contractor must accurately state the limited service: for an eligible unanswered inbound call conditionally forwarded to the approved Zenna number, Zenna sends the caller an approved SMS asking for their name, suburb, and job details, then alerts the trade business so it can respond directly. The purpose is to reduce the chance that a genuine caller is lost solely because the tradie could not answer while working.

3.4 Zenna Operations alone decides whether to decline, defer, or accept a prospect, including for capacity, technical, compliance, fit, territory, or readiness reasons. Availability is confirmed only after Zenna Operations accepts the customer and completes a controlled activation test.

## 4. Prohibited conduct and required escalation

4.1 The Contractor must not:

- take payment, issue refunds, access Stripe, or access customer credentials, Twilio, source code, or private systems;
- configure conditional call forwarding or provide technical support;
- promise a particular response time, job outcome, financial result, recovered-job rate, revenue outcome, conversion rate, financial return, universal carrier support, software integration, live person, or any capability not written in the Offer Source of Truth;
- describe Zenna as a live receptionist, AI voice agent, booking agent, quoting system, CRM integration, call centre, guaranteed booked-job service, emergency coverage, or custom telephony build;
- alter the price, billing, scope, terms, or cancellation terms;
- claim universal support for every carrier, handset, plan, forwarding configuration, or call type; or
- improvise an answer or commitment outside the Offer Source of Truth.

4.2 If a prospect asks for a promise, technical step, price change, refund, carrier-compatibility answer, or support beyond the Offer Source of Truth, the Contractor must record the question and hand it to **Zenna Operations**. The Contractor must not answer by improvisation.

4.3 The Contractor must not present conditional call forwarding as something the Contractor will perform. After payment clears, Zenna Operations guides the customer through the approved change; the customer performs any change on their own device or account.

## 5. Customer offer, handoff, and pilot boundaries

5.1 The Contractor must not offer a customer anything other than the approved offer. The customer price is **NZ$250 per month plus GST only if legally applicable**, paid monthly on a recurring basis through the official Zenna payment link.

5.2 Billing begins only after account acceptance and customer payment clearance. The customer may request month-to-month cancellation before the next billing date. There is no discretionary pro-rata refund, while statutory rights and an approved service-failure remedy remain available.

5.3 Zenna Operations alone sends the official offer and payment link, guides consent-based configuration after cleared payment, conducts and documents the controlled test, determines live status, and handles service and billing questions after handoff.

5.4 A successfully tested account is the only account considered activated. The Contractor must not promise activation or acceptance.

5.5 The Contractor must not solicit or describe the pilot as suitable for a business requiring a full phone-answering service, call centre, guaranteed booked jobs, CRM integration, quoting, dispatch, emergency coverage, or custom telephony build.

## 6. Attributed leads, pipeline records, and duplicate leads

6.1 An **Attributed Lead** for bounty purposes is a prospect that the Contractor has recorded in the Zenna pilot pipeline with the required facts, explicit consent to a Zenna Operations handoff, a relevant **unique pipeline deal ID**, and supporting evidence. Attribution is subject to the objective bounty conditions in clause 7 and Zenna Operations’ acceptance of the account.

6.2 The Contractor must create and keep accurate, contemporaneous pipeline records. At a minimum, record only the necessary information: business name, contact name, work mobile, service area, business email if volunteered, fit notes, interest/consent status, deal ID, and process milestones.

6.3 A bounty is considered only against the relevant unique pipeline deal ID and evidence. **Duplicate leads are not payable.** If records identify the same prospect more than once or attribution cannot be supported by the relevant unique deal ID and evidence, no bounty is due unless Zenna Operations confirms a qualifying unique attributed deal under the Offer Source of Truth.

6.4 Unqualified or unaccepted prospects are not payable. The Contractor must not claim a bounty merely because it made contact, created a record, arranged a handoff, or obtained interest.

## 7. Bounty: amount, milestones, and non-payment events

7.1 The total bounty is **NZ$250 per uniquely attributed, accepted pilot customer**, with no trailing commission during the five-customer pilot.

7.2 The bounty is split into the following objective milestones:

| Milestone | Amount | All objective conditions |
|---|---:|---|
| **Milestone 1** | **NZ$125** | The customer’s payment clears; Zenna Operations accepts the account; the controlled activation test passes; and no material sales misrepresentation is found. |
| **Milestone 2** | **NZ$125** | The customer remains active for 30 days with no refund, chargeback, cancellation, or material onboarding misrepresentation. |

7.3 A bounty is approved only where the relevant unique pipeline deal ID and evidence support the claim. Duplicate, unqualified, unaccepted, refunded, chargeback-affected, cancelled, or misrepresented sales are not payable. No amount is payable for a sale involving material sales misrepresentation or material onboarding misrepresentation.

7.4 For clarity, a refund, chargeback, cancellation, or material onboarding misrepresentation within the 30-day period means Milestone 2 is not payable. A material sales misrepresentation means Milestone 1 is not payable. The Contractor must promptly report any facts it learns that may affect bounty eligibility.

## 8. Intellectual property, approved materials, and data handling

8.1 Zenna retains its rights in its name, offer, approved battle card, approved positioning, operational processes, payment link, pipeline, service workflow, and other Zenna materials. The Contractor receives a limited, non-transferable, revocable permission during this engagement to use the approved acquisition materials only to perform the permitted services and only in accordance with these terms.

8.2 The Contractor must not alter, publish as its own, license, share, reverse engineer, misuse, or use Zenna materials, access, systems, or information for any purpose outside the permitted services. The Contractor must not access Twilio, source code, Stripe, customer credentials, or private systems.

8.3 The Contractor may collect and record only the minimum customer/prospect data stated in clause 6.2 and only for the permitted acquisition and handoff process. The Contractor must not request or collect passwords, account PINs, payment-card details, or unnecessary personal data.

8.4 The Contractor must keep pipeline and prospect information accurate, confidential, and limited to the approved Zenna process. The Contractor must not sell, disclose, repurpose, or use it for another client, product, or purpose. On Zenna’s request or on termination, the Contractor must promptly provide or return applicable records and must stop using Zenna materials and data, subject to any retention requirement identified by NZ legal review.

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Contractor must keep confidential all non-public information it receives or creates in connection with this engagement, including prospect/customer data, pipeline records, deal IDs, bounty information, approved materials, technical or operational information, pricing process, payment-link information, and business information.

9.2 The Contractor may use confidential information only to perform the permitted services and may disclose it only to Zenna Operations or where NZ legal review confirms disclosure is required. The Contractor must take reasonable care to prevent unauthorised access or disclosure.

## 10. Invoicing, tax, and payment administration

10.1 The Contractor is responsible for its own invoicing, business expenses, income tax, GST registration/status, ACC obligations, and other contractor obligations, except to the extent a reviewed written agreement expressly states otherwise. Zenna does not provide employment benefits or make employment-related deductions merely by engaging the Contractor as an independent contractor.

10.2 The Contractor may invoice only for bounty milestones that have become payable under clause 7 and that Zenna Operations has approved against the relevant unique pipeline deal ID and evidence. Each invoice must identify the applicable deal ID, milestone, and amount.

10.3 The parties must complete, during NZ legal review, the invoice recipient, required invoice details, payment timing, GST treatment, and any tax-withholding treatment required for the final agreement. The Contractor must not charge a customer or collect customer funds.

## 11. Term, termination, and consequences

11.1 This engagement begins on the Effective Date and continues on a month-to-month basis unless ended under this clause or replaced by a reviewed written agreement.

11.2 Either party may end the engagement by written notice. Zenna may immediately suspend or end the engagement where the Contractor breaches the Offer Source of Truth or these terms, including by misrepresentation, unauthorised payment or system access, unauthorised data handling, technical configuration, or unauthorised alteration of the offer.

11.3 On termination, the Contractor must immediately stop representing Zenna, stop using Zenna materials and access, cease acquisition activity, hand over current accurate pipeline records and relevant evidence, and comply with clauses 8 and 9.

11.4 Termination does not create an entitlement to a bounty that has not satisfied every applicable objective condition before or after termination. A previously approved milestone remains subject to the conditions in clause 7, including the 30-day requirement for Milestone 2.

## 12. Dispute path

12.1 The parties will first try in good faith to resolve any issue through written discussion between the Contractor and Zenna Operations, with the relevant pipeline deal ID and evidence where the issue concerns attribution or bounty.

12.2 If unresolved, the matter must be escalated in writing to the named Zenna decision-maker/contact to be completed following NZ legal review. Before use, a New Zealand-qualified lawyer must confirm the final dispute-resolution process, governing-law provisions, forum, notice method, and any required consumer, employment, privacy, tax, or other legal terms.
