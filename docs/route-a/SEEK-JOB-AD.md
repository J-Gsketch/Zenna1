## Ad body

### Help introduce a focused missed-call pilot to Kiwi electricians

Zenna is recruiting a **New Zealand-based independent B2B acquisition contractor** for a small pilot aimed at electrician and sparky businesses.

The pilot is designed for owner-operators who are often on the tools, driving, or otherwise unable to answer every new enquiry call. For an eligible unanswered inbound call that is conditionally forwarded to an approved Zenna number, Zenna sends the caller an approved SMS asking for their name, suburb, and job details, then alerts the trade business so it can respond directly.

This is a **remote, independent contractor** opportunity. You will identify and qualify suitable businesses, keep clean pipeline records, and arrange consent-based handoffs to Zenna Operations. The pilot is limited to the **first five accepted New Zealand electrician/sparky businesses**.

### The role

You will:

- Build prospect lists from permitted public sources.
- Start short, respectful conversations using Zenna’s approved battle card.
- Identify likely fit: New Zealand electrician/sparky businesses with **1–3 vans**, a working business mobile number, genuine inbound enquiry calls, and no dedicated receptionist.
- Capture only the necessary pipeline information: business and contact details, work mobile, service area, current missed-call process, fit notes, interest status, and explicit consent for a Zenna Operations handoff.
- Maintain accurate pipeline records and the unique deal ID for each prospect.
- Arrange Operations handoffs for qualified, consenting prospects.
- Record and escalate questions about technical steps, compatibility, pricing changes, refunds, or support to Zenna Operations.

### What you will not be asked to do

This role does **not** include taking payments, issuing refunds, accessing Stripe or customer credentials, configuring call forwarding, or providing technical support. Zenna Operations manages payment, acceptance, activation, service, and billing after handoff.

Please do not position Zenna as a receptionist, booking service, live person, quoting system, CRM integration, or custom telephony build. Do not alter the approved price, billing, scope, terms, or cancellation terms.

### How to position the pilot

Use this approved positioning in conversations:

> “Zenna is running a small missed-call text-back pilot for Kiwi electricians. If you are on a job and cannot answer, eligible calls can be conditionally forwarded to a Zenna number, which sends the caller a message asking for their name, suburb, and job details. It is not a receptionist or booking service, and availability is confirmed after a quick operations check and test. Would you be open to a short handoff with Zenna Operations to see whether it suits your current setup?”

Zenna Operations confirms availability only after it has accepted the customer and completed a controlled activation test. The customer performs any approved conditional-call-forwarding change on their own device/account, with Zenna Operations guidance after payment clearance.

### Bounty: NZ$250 total per accepted, uniquely attributed pilot customer

This is a **bounty-based contractor role**, not a salaried role. The total bounty is **NZ$250 per uniquely attributed, accepted pilot customer**, paid in two stages:

| Bounty milestone | Amount | Conditions for approval |
|---|---:|---|
| **Milestone 1** | **NZ$125** | The customer’s payment has cleared; Zenna Operations has accepted the account; the controlled activation test has passed; and no material sales misrepresentation is found. |
| **Milestone 2** | **NZ$125** | The customer remains active for 30 days with no refund, chargeback, cancellation, or material onboarding misrepresentation. |

A bounty is approved only where the relevant unique pipeline deal ID and supporting evidence are present. **Milestone 1 is not payable** for duplicate, unqualified, unaccepted, or materially misrepresented sales. **Milestone 2 is not payable** if, during its 30-day eligibility period, the customer has a refund, chargeback, cancellation, or material onboarding misrepresentation. There is **no trailing commission** during this five-customer pilot.

The pilot price you may communicate is **NZ$250 per month plus GST only if legally applicable**, billed monthly through the official Zenna payment link. Billing begins only after account acceptance and customer payment clearance; the customer can request cancellation before the next billing date. Do not take payment or change these terms.

### You may be a good fit if you

- Are based in **New Zealand** and comfortable working remotely as an independent contractor.
- Have practical B2B prospecting, lead qualification, or trade-services sales experience.
- Can have concise, respectful outreach conversations without overpromising.
- Are organised enough to maintain accurate, evidence-based pipeline records.
- Can follow an approved script and escalation process closely.
- Understand the difference between qualified interest and a completed customer activation.

### This role is not suited to someone who

- Wants to sell a full phone-answering service, call centre, booking service, CRM integration, quoting, dispatch, emergency coverage, or a custom telephony build.
- Is unwilling to use the approved positioning, price, and terms.
- Wants to handle customer payments, credentials, technical configuration, or post-handoff support.
- Relies on promises about reply speed, job outcomes, financial results, carrier compatibility, integrations, or capabilities not approved by Zenna.
