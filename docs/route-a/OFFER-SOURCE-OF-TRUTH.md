# Zenna Route A — NZ Electrician Pilot Offer

**Version:** 1.0 — operational source of truth  
**Status:** approved for pilot recruitment materials only  
**Pilot limit:** first five accepted New Zealand electrician/sparky businesses

> Every job ad, contractor briefing, candidate response, customer offer, activation record, and bounty decision must follow this document. If another file differs, this file wins.

## 1. Who this is for

**Target customer:** a New Zealand electrician/sparky business with 1–3 vans, a working business mobile number, genuine inbound enquiry calls, and no dedicated receptionist.

**Best fit:** owner-operators who are frequently on the tools, driving, or unable to answer every new customer call.

**Not a fit:** businesses requiring a full phone-answering service, a call centre, guaranteed booked jobs, CRM integration, quoting, dispatch, emergency coverage, or a custom telephony build.

## 2. What Zenna does

Zenna is a concierge missed-call text-back pilot. For an eligible unanswered inbound call that is conditionally forwarded to the approved Zenna number, Zenna sends the caller an approved SMS asking for their name, suburb, and job details. Zenna then alerts the trade business so it can respond directly.

**Pilot purpose:** reduce the chance that a genuine caller is lost solely because the tradie could not answer while working.

## 3. What Zenna does not do

Zenna does **not** promise or provide:

- a live receptionist, AI voice agent, booking agent, quoting system, or CRM integration;
- a guaranteed reply speed, recovered-job rate, revenue outcome, conversion rate, or financial return;
- universal support for every carrier, handset, plan, forwarding configuration, or call type;
- technical support from the acquisition contractor;
- customer credential access by the acquisition contractor.

Availability is confirmed only after Zenna Operations accepts the customer and completes a controlled activation test.

## 5. Customer activation path

1. **Qualified interest:** contractor records the required facts in the Zenna pilot pipeline.
2. **Acceptance:** Zenna Operations checks fit, territory, capacity, and readiness.
3. **Official offer:** Zenna Operations sends the offer and payment link.
4. **Consent-based configuration:** after payment clears, Zenna Operations guides the customer through the approved conditional-call-forwarding change. The customer performs any change on their own device/account.
5. **Controlled test:** Zenna Operations verifies the forwarding and SMS workflow with a documented test.
6. **Live status:** only a successfully tested account is considered activated.
7. **Support:** Zenna Operations handles service and billing questions after handoff.

## 6. Independent acquisition contractor role

### Allowed

- Build lists from permitted public sources.
- Start a short, respectful conversation using the approved battle card.
- Qualify fit and capture business name, contact name, mobile, service area, current missed-call process, interest status, and explicit consent to a Zenna Operations handoff.
- Keep accurate pipeline records.
- Arrange an Operations handoff.

### Prohibited

- Take payment, issue refunds, access Stripe, customer credentials, Twilio, source code, or private systems.
- Configure forwarding or provide technical support.
- Promise a specific response time, job outcome, financial result, universal carrier support, software integrations, a live person, or capability not written here.
- Alter price, billing, scope, terms, or cancellation terms.
- Represent themselves as a customer’s employee or as the founder personally.
