# Zenna SEO Roadmap: 0 → 1,000 Tradie Customers

> **Mission:** Build an organic search engine acquisition engine modeled after early-stage SaaS leaders (Stripe, Zapier, Calendly, Twilio) to capture high-intent Australian and New Zealand trade business searches.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & SEO Strategy

Zenna's SEO strategy targets high-intent, low-competition niche keywords in the trade services market (~50,000 SMB trade businesses across Australia and New Zealand). 

Unlike generic B2B SaaS companies that target broad terms ("AI call software"), Zenna dominates verticalized trade keywords ("AI receptionist for plumbers", "electrician call management system") where monthly search volume is 100–500 clicks/month, competition is low, and conversion intent is extremely high (>15% trial conversion).

### Core SEO Principles:
1. **Vertical Relevance First:** Target specific trades (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, locksmiths, drainlayers) with localized AU/NZ messaging.
2. **Intent-Driven Architecture:** Separate content into Problem-Aware (TOFU), Solution-Aware (MOFU), and Decision-Stage (BOFU) pillars.
3. **Compound Organic Traffic:** Publish 2x/week initially, building a domain authority moat that lowers Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to <$25 by Month 6.

## 5. Technical & On-Page SEO Specifications

### 1. Schema Markup Standards
All blog posts and landing pages must include JSON-LD schema:
- `SoftwareApplication` schema on commercial landing pages (`/plumbing-ai-receptionist`).
- `FAQPage` schema on guides answering common trade queries.
- `LocalBusiness` schema targeting AU/NZ trade service regions.

### 2. Core Web Vitals & Performance Rules
- **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint):** < 1.2s (Vite static rendering + optimized WebP imagery).
- **FID / INP:** < 50ms.
- **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift):** 0.0.

### 3. Internal Linking Architecture
Every blog post must include minimum 3 internal links:
1. `CTA Link` in Intro → 7-Day Free Trial Signup (`/signup`).
2. `Contextual Link` in Body → High-intent trade landing page (e.g. `/plumbing-ai-receptionist`).
3. `Related Content Link` → Relevant case study or ROI calculator.
