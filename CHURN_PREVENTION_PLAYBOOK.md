# Churn Prevention Playbook

> Identify at-risk customers early, intervene with targeted support, and recover relationships before they cancel.

---

## Churn Philosophy

**The best churn prevention is proactive value delivery.** By the time a customer asks to cancel, it's often too late. Our goal is to identify churn risk 2–4 weeks before the customer even thinks about leaving.

---

## Early Warning Signals

### Automated Alerts (Daily Scan)

| Signal | Threshold | Severity | Auto-Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| No login | 5+ days | Warning | "We Miss You" email |
| No calls answered | 3+ days | Warning | CSM check-in task |
| Call volume drop | >50% week-over-week | High | CSM call scheduled |
| Support complaint | Any negative ticket | High | CSM call within 24 hrs |
| Payment failure | 1st failure | Critical | Dunning email + CSM call |
| Feature request ignored | 30+ days old | Medium | Product team review |
| NPS detractor | Score 0–6 | High | Follow-up within 48 hrs |

### Health Score Thresholds

| Score | Status | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| 80–100 | Healthy ✅ | None — explore expansion |
| 60–79 | Monitor 👀 | Weekly check-in |
| 40–59 | At Risk ⚠️ | Intervention within 48 hrs |
| 0–39 | Critical 🚨 | Same-day founder call |

## Intervention Playbooks

### Playbook 1: Low Usage

**Trigger:** Customer hasn't logged in for 5+ days or call volume dropped significantly.

**Steps:**
1. **Day 1:** Send automated "We Miss You" email
   - Subject: "Everything okay, [Name]?"
   - Body: Highlight a new feature, share a success story, offer help
2. **Day 3:** CSM makes personal call
   - "Hey [Name], noticed Zenna hasn't been as busy lately. Everything alright on your end?"
   - Listen for: business slowdown, frustration, competitor evaluation
3. **Day 7:** If still low usage, send value recap email
   - "Here's what Zenna has done for you: [X calls answered, Y bookings, Z hours saved]"
4. **Day 14:** If no improvement, schedule business review
   - Understand root cause, adjust plan if needed

### Playbook 2: Feature Request Unfulfilled

**Trigger:** Customer requested a feature 30+ days ago that hasn't been delivered.

**Steps:**
1. Acknowledge the request: "We haven't forgotten about [feature]"
2. Provide timeline update (or explain why it's not planned)
3. Offer workaround if available
4. If critical to their business: escalate to Product Manager
5. Add to next Customer Advisory Board agenda

### Playbook 3: Price Objection

**Trigger:** Customer mentions price as a concern, or payment fails.

**Steps:**
1. Understand the real issue (cash flow? not seeing value? comparing competitors?)
2. Show ROI: "Zenna answered [X] calls worth approximately $[Y] in bookings this month"
3. Offer options:
   - Downgrade to lower tier
   - Pause subscription for 1 month
   - Annual billing discount (20% off)
   - Loyalty discount (10% off for 3 months)
4. If they still want to cancel: process gracefully, schedule exit interview

### Playbook 4: Support Frustration

**Trigger:** Negative support interaction or unresolved ticket.

**Steps:**
1. CSM calls within 24 hours: "I'm sorry about your experience with [issue]"
2. Take ownership: "This is on us. Here's what I'm doing to fix it."
3. Fix the issue (or escalate to engineering with priority tag)
4. Follow up after resolution: "Is everything working now?"
5. Log the interaction for product improvement

### Playbook 5: Competitor Evaluation

**Trigger:** Customer mentions evaluating a competitor.

**Steps:**
1. Don't panic. Ask: "What attracted you to [competitor]?"
2. Honest comparison: acknowledge competitor strengths, highlight Zenna advantages
3. Offer a competitive review call with Product Manager
4. Create a comparison document specific to their use case
5. If they leave: ask for a 30-day exit interview to learn

## Save Tactics (Last Resort)

| Tactic | When to Use | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Discount | Price objection | 20% off for 2–3 months |
| Free extension | Not enough time to evaluate | 2 weeks free |
| Plan downgrade | Over-provisioned | Move to lower tier |
| Pause subscription | Seasonal business | Pause for 1–3 months |
| Concierge setup | Technical overwhelm | We configure everything |
| Feature fast-track | Critical missing feature | Commit to delivery timeline |
| Founder call | High-value customer | CEO personally calls to save |

## Churn Recovery (Win-Back)

### 30-Day Win-Back Campaign
- **Day 1 after churn:** Exit survey (why did you leave?)
- **Day 7:** "We heard you" email (what we've improved)
- **Day 30:** "Come back" offer (discount + new features)

### 90-Day Win-Back Campaign
- **Day 90:** "What's new at Zenna" newsletter
- **Day 120:** Personal outreach from CSM
- **Day 180:** "We'd love you back" offer (30% off first 3 months)
