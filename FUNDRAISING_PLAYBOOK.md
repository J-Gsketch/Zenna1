## Fundraising Strategy

### When to Raise

| Signal | Ready? |
|---|---|
| Product-market fit (NPS 50+, churn <2%) | ✅ |
| $50K+ MRR with 20%+ MoM growth | ✅ |
| Clear path to $1M+ MRR | ✅ |
| Team of 5+ with key roles filled | ✅ |
| 18+ months runway remaining | ✅ |
| Can articulate use of funds | ✅ |

### How Much to Raise

| Round | Amount | Valuation | Use of Funds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-seed | $250–500K | $2–4M pre | MVP, first customers |
| Seed | $500K–1.5M | $4–8M pre | PMF, initial team |
| **Series A** | **$2–5M** | **$15–25M pre** | **Scale, team growth, market expansion** |
| Series B | $10–20M | $50–100M pre | Multi-market, multi-product |

### Series A Use of Funds

| Category | % | Amount ($3M raise) | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering | 40% | $1.2M | 5–8 engineers, DevOps, QA |
| Sales & Marketing | 30% | $900K | 2 AEs, marketing spend, events |
| Customer Success | 15% | $450K | 2 CSMs, support tooling |
| Operations | 10% | $300K | Ops manager, legal, accounting |
| Reserve | 5% | $150K | Unexpected expenses |

### Investor Outreach Strategy

1. **Build relationship before you need money** (6 months before raise)
2. **Monthly investor updates** (even to investors who said no — "not yet")
3. **Warm intros > cold emails** (ask advisors, other founders, customers)
4. **Create FOMO** (multiple term sheets, timeline pressure)
5. **Practice pitch 50+ times** before first VC meeting

## Pitch Deck Structure

### Slide 1: Title
- Zenna logo + tagline: "AI Receptionist for Trade Businesses"
- Contact info

### Slide 2: Problem
- Tradies miss 40% of calls (they're on the tools)
- Each missed call = $200–500 in lost revenue
- Existing solutions: answering services (expensive, low quality) or nothing

### Slide 3: Solution
- Zenna: AI receptionist that answers every call
- Qualifies leads, books jobs, collects deposits
- Australian voice, trade-specific knowledge
- Demo screenshot or video (30 seconds)

### Slide 4: Market
- TAM: $2B (global trade services communication)
- SAM: $200M (Australian + NZ trade businesses)

## Fundraising Process Timeline

### Phase 1: Preparation (4–6 weeks)
- [ ] Finalise pitch deck (iterate 10+ times)
- [ ] Build financial model (3-year projection)
- [ ] Prepare data room (see checklist below)
- [ ] Create target investor list (50+ investors)
- [ ] Get warm introductions (advisors, founders, customers)
- [ ] Practice pitch (50+ times, record yourself)

### Phase 2: Outreach (2–4 weeks)
- [ ] Send investor update email to warm network
- [ ] Request warm introductions (5–10/week)
- [ ] Follow up with intro emails
- [ ] Schedule first meetings (5–10/week)
- [ ] Track everything in CRM

### Phase 3: Meetings (4–8 weeks)
- [ ] First meetings: 30-min intro calls
- [ ] Second meetings: deep dive (60 min, product demo)
- [ ] Partner meetings: present to full partnership
- [ ] Follow up within 24 hours of every meeting
- [ ] Share data room access with interested VCs

### Phase 4: Term Sheets (2–4 weeks)
- [ ] Receive term sheet(s)
- [ ] Negotiate terms (valuation, board seat, option pool, liquidation preference)
- [ ] Get legal counsel review
- [ ] Create competitive tension (if multiple offers)
- [ ] Sign term sheet

### Phase 5: Due Diligence (4–6 weeks)
- [ ] Provide data room access
- [ ] Respond to DD questions promptly
- [ ] Customer reference calls (VC calls 3–5 customers)
- [ ] Technical DD (code review, architecture review)
- [ ] Legal DD (contracts, IP, employment)
- [ ] Financial DD (bank statements, revenue verification)

### Phase 6: Close (2–4 weeks)
- [ ] Final legal documents (SHA, SSA, board consents)
- [ ] Board approval
- [ ] Signature
- [ ] Wire transfer
- [ ] Announce! 🎉

## Key Terms to Negotiate

| Term | Founder-Friendly | Market Standard | Investor-Friendly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valuation | Higher | Market rate | Lower |
| Board seats | 1 investor, 2 founder | 1 investor, 1 founder, 1 independent | 2 investor, 1 founder |
| Liquidation preference | 1x non-participating | 1x non-participating | 1x participating |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average | Broad-based weighted average | Full ratchet |
| Option pool | Post-money (smaller) | 10–15% pre-money | 15–20% pre-money |
| Vesting | 4yr, 1yr cliff (standard) | 4yr, 1yr cliff | Accelerated |
| Pro-rata rights | Limited | Standard | Super pro-rata |
| Drag-along | Higher threshold | Standard | Lower threshold |
| Protective provisions | Minimal | Standard | Extensive |
