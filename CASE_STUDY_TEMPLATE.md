# Zenna Customer Case Study Framework & Template

> **Purpose:** Standardize the capture, formatting, and publication of customer success stories to drive maximum social proof and conversion across all marketing channels.

---

## 1. Customer Interview Protocol

### Pre-Interview Preparation:
1. Verify user metrics in Zenna dashboard (> 15 calls handled, > 3 booked jobs).
2. Send $50 coffee/tool voucher to customer for 15 minutes of their time.
3. Record interview over Zoom or ask for 60-second raw iPhone video.

### 5 Core Interview Questions:
1. **The Background:** *"What trade business do you run, where are you located, and how big is your team?"*
2. **The Bottleneck:** *"Before Zenna, what happened when your phone rang while your hands were full on site?"*
3. **The Aha Moment:** *"What made you decide to test Zenna over traditional call centers or voicemails?"*
4. **The Metrics:** *"How many extra calls or jobs did Zenna catch for you in your first month?"*
5. **The Impact:** *"What has having Zenna meant for your stress levels and family time in the evenings?"*

## 3. Sample Filled Case Study: Dave's Plumbing Co.

# Case Study: How Dave’s Plumbing Booked 3 Extra Jobs/Week On-Site in Auckland

> *"Before Zenna, I was throwing away at least $1,000 every week in missed calls while under houses. Now, Zenna picks up instantly, quotes my call-out fee, and locks in the job."*  
> — **Dave Miller**, Owner, Dave’s Plumbing Co. (Auckland, NZ)

---

### Executive Summary
- **Business Name:** Dave’s Plumbing Co.
- **Location:** Auckland, New Zealand
- **Trade Category:** Residential Plumbing & Emergency Drainage
- **Team Size:** Sole Trader + 1 Apprentice
- **Time using Zenna:** 60 Days

---

### Key Metrics & ROI Impact Card

| Metric | Before Zenna | With Zenna | Net Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Missed Calls / Week** | 8 – 12 Calls | 0 Calls | **100% Recaptured** |
| **Weekly Booked Jobs** | 5 Jobs | 8 Jobs | **+60% Growth** |
| **Monthly Revenue Impact** | $7,000 NZD | $11,500 NZD | **+$4,500 / month** |
| **Evening Admin Time** | 2 Hours / Night | 15 Mins / Night | **8.75 Hours Saved / Wk** |
| **Zenna ROI** | N/A | **30x Return** ($149 plan generated $4.5k) |

---

### The Challenge
Dave Miller runs a busy residential plumbing outfit in Auckland. Like most trade business owners, Dave spent his days with wet or dirty hands under sinks, holding heavy pipes, or operating machinery. 

When potential clients called, Dave had to make an impossible choice:
1. Stop working, wipe hands, and answer (risking safety and delaying the current job).
2. Let it go to voicemail (where 80% of customers hung up and called another plumber).

*"I checked my call logs at 5 PM every day and saw 4 or 5 missed calls from unknown numbers. When I called them back, they'd already booked someone else. It was brutal,"* says Dave.

---

### The Solution
In November 2025, Dave activated **Zenna AI Receptionist**:
- **5-Minute Setup:** Configured his business hours, suburbs served, and standard $150 call-out fee.
- **Mobile Forwarding:** Set up mobile call forwarding (*21* forwarding code) when on-site.
- **Instant SMS Text-Back:** Zenna answers calls in 3 seconds using a natural Kiwi accent, qualifies the issue, quotes the call-out fee, and texts the client Dave's calendar booking link.

---

### The Results
In his first 30 days with Zenna:
- Zenna fielded **48 total calls**.
- Automatically qualified and booked **12 extra jobs** without Dave taking a single call on-site.
- Generated an extra **$4,500 NZD in gross monthly revenue**.

*"Zenna paid for itself on day 2. If you're a tradie on the tools, not having this is literally like letting cash fall out of your pocket,"* Dave concludes.
