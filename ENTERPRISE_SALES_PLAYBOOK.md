## Demo Call Framework

### Pre-Demo Research (10 min)
- [ ] Review their website, Google reviews, social media
- [ ] Identify their service area, team size, specialities
- [ ] Check if they have online booking already
- [ ] Prepare customised talking points

### Demo Structure (30 min)

#### 1. Discovery (10 min)
| Question | Purpose |
|---|---|
| "How many calls do you get per day?" | Understand volume |
| "Who answers your phone currently?" | Identify pain point |
| "What happens to after-hours calls?" | Highlight gap |
| "How do you handle bookings and deposits?" | Understand workflow |
| "What's your average job value?" | Calculate ROI |

#### 2. Demo (10 min)
- **Live call simulation:** "Let me show you — I'll call Zenna right now as if I'm a customer"
- Show AI answering, qualifying, and booking
- Show dashboard: leads, bookings, revenue
- Show Google Calendar integration
- Show Stripe deposit collection

#### 3. ROI & Close (10 min)
- Calculate their specific ROI: "Based on [X] missed calls/week at $[Y] average job value, you're losing $[Z]/month"
- Present pricing: Starter vs. Pro comparison
- Offer pilot: "Let's do a 30-day pilot — if you don't see results, you don't pay"
- Ask for the business: "Can we get you started this week?"

### Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "My customers won't talk to a robot" | "95% of callers don't realise it's AI. Listen to this recording..." |
| "Too expensive" | "Let's look at the ROI. One recovered job pays for 3 months of Zenna." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. What specifically would you like to think over? Maybe I can address it now." |
| "We already have an answering service" | "How's that working? Zenna costs 80% less and books jobs in real-time." |
| "I'm not tech-savvy" | "That's why we exist. Setup takes 10 minutes and we do it for you." |
| "Let me talk to my partner" | "Absolutely. Can we schedule a 15-min call with both of you this week?" |
