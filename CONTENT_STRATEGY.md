# Zenna Content Strategy: Blog, Video, Email & Social Engine

> **Purpose:** Execute a high-converting content creation and repurposing engine designed to attract, educate, and convert Australian and New Zealand trade business owners (plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, locksmiths) into paying Zenna subscribers.

## 1. Content Engine Overview

The Zenna Content Engine operates on a **1 → 10 Repurposing Framework**:
Every piece of core long-form content (a 2,000-word blog post or a 7-minute YouTube video) is systematically decomposed into 10 multi-channel assets:

```
                          ┌───────────────────────────┐
                          │  CORE LONG-FORM ASSET     │
                          │ (Blog Post / YT Video)    │
                          └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                        │
             ┌──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┐
             ▼                          ▼                          ▼
   ┌───────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────┐
   │ 3 Short Videos    │      │ 1 Email Newsletter│      │ 3 Social Posts    │

   └───────────────────┘      └───────────────────┘      └───────────────────┘
```

## 2. Detailed Blog Content Specifications

### Spec 1: Problem-Aware (TOFU)
- **Title:** Why Plumbers Lose $3,000+ a Week in Missed Calls (And How to Stop)
- **Target Keyword:** `how to manage plumbing leads` (Search Vol: 210/mo)
- **Word Count:** 2,000 words
- **Tone:** Direct, no-bullshit, empathetic to on-site realities ("you're elbows deep in a pipe").
- **Outline:**
  1. The Math of a Missed Call: Average job value ($350) vs customer patience (under 3 mins).
  2. Why Voicemail Is Dead: 82% of homeowners hang up and call the next plumber on Google.
  3. The On-Site Dilemma: Safety hazards and dirty hands while handling calls.
  4. Three Fixes: Hiring a $45k/yr receptionist, using a legacy call center ($3/min), or instant AI text-back.
  5. Action Plan: 5-minute setup to stop losing leads today.
- **CTA / Lead Magnet:** Free Interactive Missed Call Loss Calculator.

### Spec 2: Solution-Aware (MOFU)
- **Title:** Zenna vs. Traditional Answering Services: 2026 Price & Speed Comparison
- **Target Keyword:** `best phone system for plumbing business` (Search Vol: 320/mo)
- **Word Count:** 1,800 words
- **Angle:** Transparent side-by-side cost and latency comparison.
- **Outline:**
  1. Overview of Legacy Answering Services (Ruby, AnswerConnect) vs. Zenna AI.
  2. Speed to Lead Test: 45 seconds (human call center transfer) vs. 2.8 seconds (Zenna AI SMS + Voice).
  3. Cost Analysis: $450/month human answering vs. $99/month Zenna flat tier.
  4. Accuracy & Customization: How Zenna learns your specific call-out fees ($150) and service area.
  5. Conclusion & Recommendation.
- **CTA:** Start 7-Day Free Trial (No Credit Card Required).

### Spec 3: Decision-Stage (BOFU)
- **Title:** Case Study: How Dave’s Plumbing Added 3 Extra Jobs/Week Without Answering His Phone
- **Target Keyword:** `AI receptionist for plumbers` (Search Vol: 250/mo)
- **Word Count:** 1,500 words

## 4. Email Newsletter Framework: "Tradie Tips Weekly"

- **Goal:** Build an owned audience of 500+ trade business owners by Week 12.
- **Frequency:** Every Tuesday at 6:30 AM (before tradies head to job sites).
- **Structure (500 words total):**
  1. **The 60-Second Tip:** Tactical business advice (e.g. "How to price emergency call-outs").
  2. **Tradie Spotlight:** 2-sentence win from a Zenna user ("Dave booked a $2.4k commercial job via Zenna last Thursday").
  3. **Product Update / Feature Highlight:** Short GIF showing a feature (e.g. Google Calendar auto-sync).
  4. **P.S. CTA:** "Want Zenna to handle your calls this week? Start 7-day trial."
