# Zenna Partner & Affiliate Program Playbook

> **Mission:** Build a high-yield partner ecosystem leveraging trade business coaches, industry influencers, software integrators, and YouTube creators to scale Zenna to 500+ paying subscribers.

---

## 1. Commission Structure & Incentive Tiers

Zenna offers a competitive recurring revenue-share model designed to generate long-term passive income for partners while maintaining healthy SaaS margins.

### Tiered Affiliate Structure

| Partner Tier | Active Referrals | Recurring Commission | Bonus Incentives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🥈 **Silver Partner** | 1 – 5 Tradies | **20% Monthly Recurring** (12 months) | Free Zenna Pro Account for own business |
| 🥇 **Gold Partner** | 6 – 20 Tradies | **25% Monthly Recurring** (12 months) | $250 Cash Milestone Bonus + Priority Support |
| 💎 **Platinum Partner** | 21+ Tradies | **30% Lifetime Recurring** | Co-branded Landing Page + Dedicated Manager |

*Example Payout:* A Gold Partner referring 15 Pro Tradies ($249/mo) earns **$933.75 / month ($11,205 / year)** in passive income.

1. **Trade Business Coaches & Consultants:** Consultants advising plumbers/electricians on scaling operations and pricing.

## 5. Terms of Service & Anti-Fraud Guidelines

1. **Self-Referral Prohibition:** Partners cannot earn commission on their own business account.
2. **PCC Search Bidding Rules:** Partners are prohibited from bidding on brand keywords (`"Zenna"`, `"Zenna AI"`) on Google Ads.
3. **Payout Terms:** Automated monthly payouts processed via **Stripe Connect** or **PayPal** on the 15th of each month (30-day clearing window).
